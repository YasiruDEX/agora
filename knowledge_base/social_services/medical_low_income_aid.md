# Medical Low-Income Aid - Social Services Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** A targeted welfare assistance program providing financial grants, surgical/treatment subsidies, and free assistive medical equipment to low-income residents facing catastrophic health expenditure. The program covers life-saving surgeries (cardiac, renal, cancer), chronic disease management (dialysis, chemotherapy), and the provision of mobility aids (wheelchairs, crutches), sensory aids (hearing aids, eyeglasses), and prosthetic devices (artificial limbs). Funding sources may include the Riverside County Department of Social Services (Riverside County DSS) discretionary welfare budget, the County Indigent Care Fund, or the "Riverside Health Safety Net" program.
- **Governing Laws & Directives:**
    - Riverside County Social Services Code and subsidiary Welfare Regulations.
    - Riverside County DSS and California Department of Public Health Joint Bulletins on Medical Welfare Assistance.
    - County Indigent Care Fund Directives for critical surgical interventions and specialized equipment.
    - Riverside County DSS Medical Welfare Assistance Guidelines and County Health By-laws.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Income Level:** The total gross monthly household income must fall below the officially designated low-income poverty threshold (generally **$1,800 to $3,200 per month** for a household of one to four, adjusted annually) as verified by the assigned County Caseworker. Priority is given to households with no fixed income, day-laborers, and households lacking any form of existing medical insurance coverage. Ownership of significant disposable assets will render the applicant ineligible.
- **Medical Condition:** The patient must be diagnosed with a critical, life-threatening, or chronic debilitating condition including but not limited to:
    - Cardiac surgeries (CABG, valve replacements, stenting).
    - End-stage renal disease requiring dialysis or transplant.
    - Oncological conditions (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgical oncology).
    - Major orthopedic or neuro-surgical procedures.
    - Physical disability requiring assistive devices (paralysis, amputation, severe hearing/visual impairment).
- **Residency:** The applicant/patient must be a permanent resident within Riverside County for a continuous period of not less than one year immediately preceding the application.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents must be compiled and submitted:
- Duly completed **Medical Assistance Application Form** (available at the Riverside County DSS Social Services Section).
- Clear copy of the **patient's Driver's License or State ID number** or **Birth Certificate** (for patients under 16 years of age).
- **County Residency & Income Certificate** containing:
    - Certification of household composition and monthly income.
    - Confirmation of permanent residence period.
    - Statement of character and economic vulnerability.
- **Original Medical Report and Cost Estimate** issued on the official letterhead of the treating Riverside County Medical Center Consultant/Specialist. The report must state:
    - Definitive diagnosis (ICD classification if available).
    - Recommended treatment plan/surgical procedure.
    - Itemized estimate of hospital charges, implant costs, and post-operative care expenses.
- **Original Proforma Invoice / Quotation** from an approved medical equipment supplier or hospital prosthetic/orthotic workshop (mandatory for wheelchair, hearing aid, or artificial limb requests).
- **Copy of Bank Statement** (first page showing account holder name and account number) for electronic fund transfer. The account must be in the name of the patient or the primary caregiver/guardian.
- Copies of any previous **Prescribed Medicine Receipts** or laboratory investigation reports (to support chronic disease fund requests).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition & Medical Endorsement**
    Collect the Medical Assistance Application Form from the Social Services & Health Welfare Branch of the Riverside County DSS office on Main St, Riverside, or download it from the official Riverside County DSS web portal. The applicant must then have the relevant Riverside County Medical Center Consultant/Specialist accurately fill and seal the "Medical Diagnosis & Cost Estimate" section of the form.

- **Step 2: County Caseworker Income Certification**
    Submit the partially completed form (with medical section filled) to the assigned County Caseworker. The caseworker conducts a household visit to verify economic status, endorses the income declaration, and countersigns the form with the official DSS seal. A negative caseworker report on income status will result in outright rejection.

- **Step 3: File Lodgment with Social Services Officer (SSO)**
    Lodge the fully endorsed application form together with all mandatory attachments with the Riverside County Social Services Officer (SSO). The SSO registers the application, assigns a welfare file number, and conducts a supplementary field investigation to verify the medical and social distress claims.

- **Step 4: Committee Evaluation & Approval**
    The SSO prepares a submission note and tables the file before the **Riverside County Medical Welfare Committee** or the General Welfare Committee. The Committee, chaired by the County Social Services Director, evaluates the case against the severity matrix and the available allocated welfare budget/Indigent Care Fund quota and issues the approval decision with a sanctioned amount.

- **Step 5: Disbursement of Aid**
    **For Financial Aid:** The approved grant is transferred directly to the patient's nominated bank account via the County Electronic Payment Platform. A Payment Voucher is issued to the patient for hospital admission.
    **For Medical Equipment:** The beneficiary receives a Letter of Authorization for collection of the equipment from the pre-approved county supplier, or the equipment is handed over at a scheduled county distribution event. The beneficiary must sign the Goods Received Register.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Fee:** Completely free of charge. No processing fee is levied at any stage.
- **Assistance Limit:** The grant amount is not fixed and varies based on medical severity and annual fiscal allocations.
    - Outpatient/Chronic treatment subsidy: $250 to $750.
    - Assistive devices (wheelchairs/hearing aids): $750 to $2,500.
    - Critical surgeries (heart/kidney/cancer): $5,000 to $25,000 (aggregate limit from multiple county fund sources).
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:**
    - Routine Medical Aid (equipment, chronic care): 14 to 30 working days from complete file submission.
    - Emergency Life-Saving Cases (certified "Urgent" by the treating Consultant): 7 working days. Emergency files must be flagged for priority processing.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Social Services & Health Welfare Branch, Riverside County DSS.
- **Supervisory Officer:** Social Services Officer (SSO) / Assistant Director of Social Services (Welfare).
- **Contact Email:** socialservices@riversidecounty.gov
- **Office Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (excluding federal holidays).
- **Escalation Grievance:** If a critical care application is not processed within the emergency SLA or is arbitrarily rejected without a written deficiency memorandum, the applicant may appeal directly to the **Riverside County Social Services Director** or escalate the matter to the **California Department of Social Services Regional Office**. For Indigent Care Fund related delays, the complaint may be directed to the **Riverside County Executive Office (Indigent Care Fund Unit)**.
