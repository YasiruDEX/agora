# Medical Low-Income Aid - Social Services Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** A targeted welfare assistance program providing financial grants, surgical/treatment subsidies, and free assistive medical equipment to low-income citizens facing catastrophic health expenditure. The scheme covers life-saving surgeries (cardiac, renal, cancer), chronic disease management (dialysis, chemotherapy), and the provision of mobility aids (wheelchairs, crutches), sensory aids (hearing aids, spectacles), and prosthetic devices (artificial limbs). Funding sources may include the Divisional Secretariat's decentralized welfare budget, the President's Fund, or the "Agrahara" health insurance safety net.
- **Governing Laws & Directives:**
    - Social Services Act and subsidiary Welfare Regulations.
    - Ministry of Health and Ministry of Social Empowerment & Welfare Joint Circulars on Medical Welfare Assistance.
    - President's Fund Directives for critical surgical interventions and specialized equipment.
    - Divisional Secretariat Medical Welfare Assistance Guidelines and Local Authority Health By-laws.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Income Level:** The total gross monthly family income must fall below the officially designated low-income poverty threshold as verified by the Grama Niladhari. Priority is given to families with no fixed income, daily-wage earners, and households lacking any form of existing medical insurance cover. Ownership of significant disposable assets will render the applicant ineligible.
- **Medical Condition:** The patient must be diagnosed with a critical, life-threatening, or chronic debilitating condition including but not limited to:
    - Cardiac surgeries (CABG, valve replacements, stenting).
    - End-stage renal disease requiring dialysis or transplant.
    - Oncological conditions (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, surgical oncology).
    - Major orthopedic or neuro-surgical procedures.
    - Physical disability requiring assistive devices (paralysis, amputation, severe hearing/visual impairment).
- **Residency:** The applicant/patient must be a permanent resident within the administrative jurisdiction of the Divisional Secretariat or Local Authority for a continuous period of not less than one year immediately preceding the application.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents must be compiled and submitted:
- Duly completed **Medical Assistance Application Form** (available at the Social Services Section).
- Clear copy of the **Patient's National Identity Card (NIC)** or **Birth Certificate** (for patients under 16 years of age).
- **Grama Niladhari (GN) Service Certificate** containing:
    - Certification of household composition and monthly income.
    - Confirmation of permanent residence period.
    - Statement of character and economic vulnerability.
- **Original Medical Report and Cost Estimate** issued on the official letterhead of the treating Government Hospital Consultant/Specialist. The report must state:
    - Definitive diagnosis (ICD classification if available).
    - Recommended treatment plan/surgical procedure.
    - Itemized estimate of hospital charges, implant costs, and post-operative care expenses.
- **Original Proforma Invoice / Quotation** from an approved medical equipment supplier or hospital prosthetic/orthotic workshop (mandatory for wheelchair, hearing aid, or artificial limb requests).
- **Copy of Bank Passbook** (first page showing account holder name and account number) for electronic fund transfer. The account must be in the name of the patient or the primary caregiver/guardian.
- Copies of any previous **Prescribed Medicine Receipts** or laboratory investigation reports (to support chronic disease fund requests).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition & Medical Endorsement**
    Collect the Medical Assistance Application Form from the Social Services & Health Welfare Branch of the Divisional Secretariat or download it from the official Divisional Secretariat web portal. The applicant must then have the relevant Government Hospital Consultant/Specialist accurately fill and seal the "Medical Diagnosis & Cost Estimate" section of the form.

- **Step 2: Grama Niladhari (GN) Income Certification**
    Submit the partially completed form (with medical section filled) to the area Grama Niladhari. The GN conducts a household visit to verify economic status, endorses the income declaration, and countersigns the form with the official GN seal. A GN negative report on income status will result in outright rejection.

- **Step 3: File Lodgment with Social Services Officer (SSO)**
    Lodge the fully endorsed application form together with all mandatory attachments with the Divisional Social Services Officer (SSO). The SSO registers the application, assigns a welfare file number, and conducts a supplementary field investigation to verify the medical and social distress claims.

- **Step 4: Committee Evaluation & Approval**
    The SSO prepares a submission note and tables the file before the **Divisional Medical Welfare Committee** or the General Welfare Committee. The Committee, chaired by the Divisional Secretary, evaluates the case against the severity matrix and the available allocated welfare budget/President's Fund quota and issues the approval decision with a sanctioned amount.

- **Step 5: Disbursement of Aid**
    **For Financial Aid:** The approved grant is transferred directly to the patient’s nominated bank account via the Government Electronic Payment Platform. A Payment Voucher is issued to the patient for hospital admission.
    **For Medical Equipment:** The beneficiary receives a Letter of Authorization for collection of the equipment from the pre-approved government supplier, or the equipment is handed over at a ceremonial divisional distribution event. The beneficiary must sign the Goods Received Register.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Fee:** Completely free of charge. No processing fee is levied at any stage.
- **Assistance Limit:** The grant quantum is not fixed and varies based on medical severity and annual fiscal allocations.
    - Outpatient/Chronic treatment subsidy: LKR 10,000 to LKR 25,000.
    - Assistive devices (wheelchairs/hearing aids): LKR 25,000 to LKR 75,000.
    - Critical surgeries (heart/kidney/cancer): LKR 100,000 to LKR 500,000 (aggregate limit from multiple state fund sources).
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:**
    - Routine Medical Aid (equipment, chronic care): 14 to 30 working days from complete file submission.
    - Emergency Life-Saving Cases (certified "Urgent" by the treating Consultant): 7 working days. Emergency files must be flagged with a red sticker for priority processing.

- **Supervisory Officer:** Social Services Officer (SSO) / Assistant Divisional Secretary (Welfare).
- **Contact Email:** socialservices@ds.gov.lk
- **Office Hours:** Weekdays (Monday to Friday), 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Closed on Public Holidays.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If a critical care application is not processed within the emergency SLA or is arbitrarily rejected without a written deficiency memorandum, the applicant may appeal directly to the **Divisional Secretary** or escalate the matter to the **Provincial Director of Social Services**. For President's Fund related delays, the complaint may be directed to the **Presidential Secretariat (President's Fund Unit)**.
