# Public Assistance Allowance - Social Services Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** A monthly financial assistance scheme designed to provide a safety net for Riverside County residents facing severe economic hardship. The program targets low-income households, destitute individuals, persons with disabilities, and widows/widowers who lack a steady source of income to meet basic subsistence requirements.
- **Governing Laws:** The scheme operates under the broader framework of the Riverside County Social Services Code and related Welfare Benefit Regulations administered by the Riverside County Department of Social Services (Riverside County DSS). Implementation at the local level is governed by bulletins issued by the California Department of Social Services, with coordination through Riverside County DSS's Social Services Division and county assistance by-laws where applicable.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Economic Threshold:** The combined gross monthly income of the family unit must fall below the official county poverty threshold (typically assessed between **$1,200 and $2,000 per month**, subject to periodic revision by the Riverside County DSS). Applicants must not own significant fixed assets (land/housing exceeding basic shelter needs) that could generate income.
- **Residency Requirement:** The applicant must be a permanent resident within Riverside County for a continuous period of no less than one year immediately preceding the application.
- **Social Vulnerability:** The head of the household must fall into one of the following categories:
    - Incapacitated due to a chronic non-communicable disease (NCD) or permanent disability that prevents gainful employment.
    - Senior residents (aged 65 years or above) with no family support.
    - Widows/widowers or single parents with dependent children and no steady income.
- **Exclusion:** Families receiving other major state or federal welfare subsidies (e.g., CalWORKs, SSI) may be subject to means-testing prioritization, though concurrent receipt is possible only if specifically approved by the County Welfare Committee.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents must be submitted in support of the application:
- Completed **Public Assistance Application Form** (Riverside County DSS format).
- Certified copy of the applicant's **Driver's License or State ID number**.
- **County Residency & Income Certificate** containing:
    - Verification of household composition.
    - Income and asset declaration (property/extent).
    - Character and residence confirmation (dated within the last 3 months).
- **Medical Certificates** issued by a Consultant/Specialist at a Riverside County Medical Center (valid for chronic illness/disability claims), specifying the degree of incapacitation and inability to work.
- Certified copies of **Birth Certificates** for all dependent children under 18 years of age.
- Certified copy of **Death Certificate of Spouse** (mandatory for widow/widower applicants).
- **Recent Passport-sized Photograph** of the applicant (2 copies).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Application Intake**
    The applicant or a nominated family member must collect the prescribed application form from the Social Services Division at the Riverside County DSS office. Community outreach officers can also provide guidance on form collection.

- **Step 2: County Caseworker Verification**
    The filled form must be presented to the assigned County Caseworker. The caseworker is legally obligated to conduct a preliminary field check, countersign the income/residence declaration, and attach a formal report assessing the socio-economic veracity of the claim. **Submission without the caseworker's seal and report is void.**

- **Step 3: Field Investigation & Casework**
    The Social Services Officer (SSO), often accompanied by the Case Manager or a County Public Health Inspector in medical cases, conducts a home-based field investigation. The SSO prepares a Social Investigation Report (SIR) documenting the family's living conditions, household resources, and actual economic mobility.

- **Step 4: Committee Review & Approval**
    The completed file is tabled at the monthly **Social Welfare Committee** meeting (or County Poverty Alleviation Committee). The committee, chaired by the County Social Services Director, scrutinizes the SSO field report against the annual quota/allocated funds and approves or rejects the application. The Social Services Director's signature is the final authorization.

- **Step 5: Enrolment & Disbursement**
    Approved beneficiaries are entered into the permanent Welfare Payment Register. A unique beneficiary number and a **Public Assistance Payment Card** are issued. Payment is disbursed via direct deposit or an authorized Riverside County DSS payment kiosk, typically using a signature verification system.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Fee:** No charge. The service is entirely free of cost.
- **Monthly Allowance Amount:** $300 to $650 per family. The exact amount is determined by the severity of destitution and the availability of the annual county budget allocation ceiling for Riverside County DSS.
- **Processing Time (SLA):** The standard Service Level Agreement requires a decision within 30 to 60 calendar days from the date of submission of the completed caseworker-verified file, dependent on the scheduling of the Welfare Committee sitting.
- **Renewal Cycle:** Assistance is not permanent; annual re-assessment of the household's economic status is mandatory to continue receiving the benefit.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Social Services & Welfare Division, Riverside County DSS.
- **Key Officials:** Social Services Officer (SSO) / County Caseworker.
- **Contact Email:** socialservices@riversidecounty.gov
- **Office Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (excluding federal holidays).
- **Escalation Grievance:** If the application is unduly delayed beyond 60 days without written reason from the SSO, a written appeal may be lodged directly with the Assistant Director of Social Services (Benefits & Welfare).
