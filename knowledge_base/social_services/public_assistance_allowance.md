# Public Assistance Allowance - Social Services Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** A monthly financial assistance scheme designed to provide a safety net for Sri Lankan citizens facing severe economic hardship. The program targets low-income households, destitute individuals, persons with disabilities, and widows who lack a steady source of income to meet basic subsistence requirements.
- **Governing Laws:** The scheme operates under the broader framework of the Social Services Act and related Welfare Benefit Regulations administered by the Department of Social Services. Implementation at the local level is governed by circulars issued by the Ministry of Social Empowerment & Welfare, with coordination through the Divisional Secretariat's Social Services Division and local authority assistance by-laws where applicable.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Economic Threshold:** The combined gross monthly income of the family unit must fall below the official government poverty threshold (typically assessed between LKR 6,000 and LKR 10,000 per month, subject to periodic revision by the Department of Census and Statistics). Applicants must not own significant fixed assets (land/housing exceeding basic shelter needs) that could generate income.
- **Residency Requirement:** The applicant must be a permanent resident within the Grama Niladhari Division for a continuous period of no less than one year immediately preceding the application.
- **Social Vulnerability:** The head of the household must fall into one of the following categories:
    - Incapacitated due to a chronic non-communicable disease (NCD) or permanent disability that prevents gainful employment.
    - Elderly citizens (aged 60 years or above) with no family support.
    - Widows or single parents with dependent children and no steady income.
- **Exclusion:** Families receiving other major state welfare subsidies (e.g., Samurdhi, Elderly Allowance) may be subject to means-testing prioritization, though concurrent receipt is possible only if specifically approved by the Divisional Welfare Committee.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents must be submitted in support of the application:
- Completed **Public Assistance Application Form** (Social Services Department format).
- Certified copy of the applicant's **National Identity Card (NIC)**.
- **Grama Niladhari (GN) Service Certificate** containing:
    - Verification of household composition.
    - Income and asset declaration (land/extent).
    - Character and residence confirmation (dated within the last 3 months).
- **Medical Certificates** issued by a Consultant/Specialist at a Government Hospital (valid for chronic illness/disability claims), specifying the degree of incapacitation and inability to work.
- Certified copies of **Birth Certificates** for all dependent children under 18 years of age.
- Certified copy of **Death Certificate of Spouse** (mandatory for widow applicants).
- **Recent Passport-sized Photograph** of the applicant (2 copies).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Application Intake**
    The applicant or a nominated family member must collect the prescribed application form from the Social Services Division at the respective Divisional Secretariat. Village-level community officers can also provide guidance on form collection.

- **Step 2: Grama Niladhari (GN) Verification**
    The filled form must be presented to the area Grama Niladhari. The GN is legally obligated to conduct a preliminary field check, countersign the income/residence declaration, and attach a formal GN report assessing the socio-economic veracity of the claim. **Submission without the GN seal and report is void.**

- **Step 3: Field Investigation & Casework**
    The Social Services Officer (SSO), often accompanied by the Development Officer or a Public Health Inspector (PHI) in medical cases, conducts a home-based field investigation. The SSO prepares a Social Investigation Report (SIR) documenting the family’s living conditions, kitchen equipment, and actual economic mobility.

- **Step 4: Committee Review & Approval**
    The completed file is tabled at the monthly **Social Welfare Committee** meeting (or Divisional Poverty Alleviation Committee). The committee, chaired by the Divisional Secretary, scrutinizes the SSO field report against the annual quota/allocated funds and approves or rejects the application. The Divisional Secretary’s signature is the final authorization.

- **Step 5: Enrolment & Disbursement**
    Approved beneficiaries are entered into the permanent Welfare Payment Register. A unique beneficiary number and a **Public Assistance Payment Card (Blue/Yellow Card)** are issued. Payment is collected from the Divisional Secretariat Shroff Counter or authorized Cooperative outlets, typically using a signature/thumbprint verification system.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Fee:** No charge. The service is entirely free of cost.
- **Monthly Allowance Amount:** LKR 2,500 to LKR 5,000 per family. The exact quantum is determined by the severity of destitution and the availability of the annual state budget allocation ceiling for the Divisional Secretary’s Division.
- **Processing Time (SLA):** The standard Service Level Agreement requires a decision within 30 to 60 calendar days from the date of submission of the completed GN-verified file, dependent on the scheduling of the Welfare Committee sitting.
- **Renewal Cycle:** Assistance is not permanent; annual re-assessment of the household’s economic status is mandatory to continue receiving the benefit.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Social Services & Welfare Division, Divisional Secretariat.
- **Key Officials:** Social Services Officer (SSO) / Grama Niladhari (GN).
- **Contact Email:** social@council.gov.lk
- **Office Hours:** Weekdays (Monday to Friday) from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Public holidays excluded.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If the application is unduly delayed beyond 60 days without written reason from the SSO, a written appeal may be lodged directly with the Additional Divisional Secretary (Land & Welfare).
