# Senior Citizen Assistance - Social Services Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: A comprehensive social security framework providing official identity certification, monthly welfare allowances, mobility and medical equipment grants, legal protection, and recreational opportunities for citizens aged 60 years and above. The service aims to ensure dignity, financial stability, social inclusion, and protection from neglect and abuse for the elderly population within the Divisional Secretariat area.
- **Governing Laws & Directives**:
    - Protection of the Rights of Elders Act No. 9 of 2000.
    - Protection of the Rights of Elders (Amendment) Act No. 5 of 2011.
    - National Secretariat for Elders (NSE) Establishment Regulations and Operational Guidelines.
    - Divisional Secretariat Circulars on Elders' Welfare Committees (*Wedahiti Samithi*) and Maintenance of Parents.
    - Public Administration Circulars on the Monthly Elders Allowance (*Wedahiti Demapiya Padi*) Scheme.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Senior Citizen Identity Card (Elders ID)**:
    - Any Sri Lankan citizen aged **60 years or above**.
    - Must be a permanent resident within the Divisional Secretariat division.
    - No income threshold; all elders are eligible for this identity document.
- **Monthly Elders Welfare Allowance (*Wedahiti Demapiya Padi*)**:
    - Must hold a valid Senior Citizen Identity Card.
    - Must be aged **70 years or above** (age threshold may vary based on prevailing national policy circulars).
    - Must fall below the official low-income / poverty line as certified by the Grama Niladhari.
    - Must not be in receipt of any government pension (including Armed Forces, Police, or State Administrative Service pensions) or the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) pension. (Elderly Samurdhi beneficiaries who meet the age criteria are eligible).
    - Must not have adult children financially capable of providing support (subject to GN/SSO verification).
- **Elder Welfare Grants & Equipment**:
    - Must be a registered member of a recognized village-level *Wedahiti Samithiya* (Elders' Committee).
    - Priority for elders with chronic illnesses, disabilities, or those abandoned by family.
- **Legal Protection (Elders Maintenance & Abuse Prevention)**:
    - Any elder facing neglect, abandonment, financial exploitation, or physical/emotional abuse by family members, caregivers, or third parties is eligible to seek intervention, irrespective of income or age (minimum 60 years).

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
- **For Senior Citizen Identity Card (New & Renewal)**:
    - Duly completed "Senior Citizen ID Application Form" (NSE Form).
    - Original Birth Certificate or Certified Extract. If the original is unavailable or destroyed, an official Birth Search Report from the Registrar General's Department or Divisional Secretariat.
    - Copy of National Identity Card (NIC). (For elders without a NIC, alternative photographic ID or attestation from Grama Niladhari).
    - Original **Grama Niladhari (GN) Certificate of Residence** confirming continuous residence in the division.
    - Two (2) recent passport-sized colour photographs (35mm x 45mm, white background).
- **For Monthly Elders Welfare Allowance (*Wedahiti Demapiya Padi*)**:
    - Copy of the valid Senior Citizen Identity Card (front and back).
    - Original **Grama Niladhari (GN) Income & Family Status Certificate**, specifically declaring:
        - Total household income falls below the threshold.
        - Names and occupations of all adult children living in the same household or elsewhere.
        - Confirmation that the elder is not receiving a government pension.
    - Photocopy of the applicant's National Savings Bank (NSB) or licensed commercial bank passbook (account in the elder's name).
- **For Legal Protection / Maintenance Complaints**:
    - Written complaint letter or formal complaint form to the Elders Rights Officer.
    - Copy of the elder's Senior Citizen Identity Card.
    - Any available evidence (e.g., medical reports in case of abuse, financial documents showing exploitation, GN statement).
- **For Special Welfare Grants / Assistive Equipment**:
    - Recommendation letter from the President/Secretary of the registered *Wedahiti Samithiya*.
    - Medical recommendation from a Government Medical Officer (if applying for wheelchairs, spectacles, hearing aids).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **A. For Senior Citizen Identity Card**:
    - **Step 1**: Obtain the Senior Citizen ID Application Form from the **Social Services / Elders Welfare Branch** at the Divisional Secretariat, the area **Grama Niladhari**, or download from the National Secretariat for Elders (NSE) website.
    - **Step 2**: Affix photographs, attach all required documents, and submit the completed file to the area **Grama Niladhari (GN)** or directly to the **Elders Rights Officer (ERO)** at the Divisional Secretariat.
    - **Step 3**: The Social Services Officer (SSO) / ERO verifies the age computation based on the Birth Certificate and cross-checks residency with the GN certificate.
    - **Step 4**: Verified applications are batch-forwarded by the Divisional Secretariat to the **National Secretariat for Elders (NSE)** for secure card printing and lamination.
    - **Step 5**: Once printed cards are received back, the SSO/ERO distributes the official **Senior Citizen Identity Card** to the applicant directly at the DS Office, or via the Grama Niladhari, or at a *Wedahiti Samithi* gathering.
- **B. For Monthly Elders Welfare Allowance**:
    - **Step 1**: Once the Senior Citizen ID is obtained, collect the specific "Monthly Allowance Application" from the GN or ERO.
    - **Step 2**: Secure the GN Income & Family Status Certificate and attach the required bank passbook copy.
    - **Step 3**: Submit the application to the ERO/SSO.
    - **Step 4**: The ERO conducts field verification (household visit) to confirm socio-economic status.
    - **Step 5**: The name is placed on the Divisional Secretariat's approved waiting list/payment queue, subject to vacancy under the allocated national quota.
    - **Step 6**: Once a slot is allocated, the monthly payment is credited directly to the bank account through Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT).

## 5. Benefits, Fees & Timelines
- **Application Fee**: All services are **entirely Free of Charge**. No fee is collected at any stage for forms, ID printing, or processing.
- **Key Benefits of Senior Citizen Identity Card**:
    - Official proof of age for all government and private sector concessions.
    - Priority queue access at Government Hospitals, Divisional Secretariats, and Licensed Commercial Banks.
    - Statutory concessions on public transport (SLTB and Private Bus fares).
    - Discounts on prescribed medical tests and medications at specified institutions.
    - Serves as primary identification for filing complaints under the Protection of the Rights of Elders Act (regarding neglect or abuse).
- **Monthly Elders Allowance Amount**:
    - Allowance value ranges from **LKR 2,000.00 to LKR 5,000.00 per month** based on prevailing national budget decisions and NSE directives.
    - The number of beneficiaries is strictly capped by the annual state welfare quota per DS division; placement on a waiting list is common.
- **Service Timelines (SLA)**:
    - *Senior Citizen Identity Card Issuance*: **14 to 30 working days** from submission of complete and verified application. (Delays may occur at the centralized printing facility of NSE).
    - *Monthly Allowance Approval & First Payment*: Conditional on vacancy availability; processing of application files takes approximately **21 working days**, but actual benefit commencement is subject to quota release by the Treasury and NSE.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division**: Elders Welfare & Disability Services Section, Social Services Branch.
- **Designated Supervisory Officers**:
    - Elders Rights Officer (ERO).
    - Social Services Officer (SSO).
- **Contact Email**: elderswelfare@ds.gov.lk
- **Physical Counter**: Elders Counter, Divisional Secretariat Main Office.
- **Office Hours**: Weekdays 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM (Direct inquiries before 2:30 PM recommended).
- **Grievance Redressal & Escalation**:
    - **Service Delays / Non-receipt of ID or Allowance**: File a written inquiry with the **Assistant Divisional Secretary (Social Services)**.
    - **Abuse, Neglect, or Exploitation Complaints**: The Elders Rights Officer (ERO) is the legally mandated First Contact Officer. The ERO has the power to summon offending adult children, mediate maintenance disputes, and refer the case to the District Elders Welfare Board.
    - **Escalation to District/National Level**: If the complaint is not satisfactorily resolved at the DS level within 14 days, escalate to the **Divisional Secretary**, and subsequently to the **National Secretariat for Elders** Head Office in Colombo, or the District-level *Elders Protection Committee* chaired by the District Secretary.
