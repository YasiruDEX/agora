# Solid Waste Management - Health & Environment Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: Systematic and scheduled collection, source-segregated transport, treatment, and final disposal of solid waste generated from households, commercial establishments, institutions, and public spaces within Riverside County limits. This service encompasses door-to-door collection, public bin maintenance, street sweeping, drain cleaning, and the operation of transfer stations and recycling centers.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances**:
    - California Integrated Waste Management Act (as amended).
    - Riverside County Ordinance Code (as amended).
    - Riverside County Municipal Solid Waste Management By-laws.
    - California Superior Court Determinations and Orders regarding Mandatory Waste Segregation and Environmental Protection.
    - County Notifications on Prohibition of Open Dumping and Burning.

## 2. Segregation Rules & Collection Guidelines
- **Source Segregation Mandate**: All households, commercial entities, and institutions are legally bound to separate waste at the point of generation into three distinct streams. **Unsegregated waste will be rejected and not collected** by the collection crew. Persistent non-compliance may result in a fine and removal of collection service.
- **Category 1: Organic / Perishable / Biodegradable (Designated Color: Green Bin or Green Bag)**
    - *Examples*: Kitchen and food scraps, fruit/vegetable peels, leftover cooked food (drained), garden clippings, leaves, flowers, market and seafood stall waste.
    - *Collection Frequency*: **3 to 5 times per week**. (Daily collection for commercial/market areas; alternate day for low-density residential).
    - *Disposal Chain*: Composting facility, biogas digester, or sanitary landfill dedicated cell.
- **Category 2: Recyclable / Dry / Non-Biodegradable (Designated Color: Blue Bin or Clear Transparent Bag)**
    - *Examples*: Clean paper and cardboard, clean plastics (PET bottles, containers), plastic sheets/bags (clean), glass bottles and jars (rinsed), aluminum and tin cans, scrap metal.
    - *Collection Frequency*: **Once a week** (specific day published by route/zone). Residents must rinse and dry containers before bagging.
    - *Disposal Chain*: Material Recovery Facility (MRF) for further sorting and sale to recyclers.
- **Category 3: Hazardous Household Waste & E-Waste (Designated: Red Bag / Rigid Container or Special Handling)**
    - *Examples*: Used batteries, CFL and LED bulbs, fluorescent tubes, expired medicines, chemical/pesticide containers, used syringes (sharps containers), e-waste (computer parts, mobile phones, chargers, cartridges).
    - *Collection Method*: **NOT ACCEPTED in daily collection vehicles.**
        - Hand over to designated mobile collection trucks announced via public notice.
        - Deposit at fixed E-waste bins located at the Riverside County DSS main office or public library.
        - Held for special quarterly/monthly collection drives.

## 3. Special & Commercial Services
- **Bulk & Garden Waste Collection (On-Call Service)**
    - Designed for waste exceeding normal household bin capacity, such as tree trimmings, palm fronds, post-construction debris (non-hazardous), furniture, and large volumes of green waste.
    - This is a paid service. Applicant must submit a written request to the Health & Cleansing Division.
- **Commercial & Institutional High-Volume Waste Management**
    - Mandatory Registration: All hotels, restaurants, banquet halls, supermarkets, hospitals, banks, and apartment complexes generating over 20 lbs of waste/day must register as a "High-Volume Generator."
    - These entities are not covered by the standard residential rates. They must enter a formal agreement.
- **E-Waste Special Collection Drives**
    - Quarterly public collection drives (Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct) managed jointly with CalEPA-registered e-waste recyclers. Dates are announced via county notice boards, official website, and community bulletins.

## 4. Application Process for Commercial Waste Service
- **Step 1**: Obtain the "Commercial Waste Collection Service Agreement" form from the Health, Sanitation & Cleansing Division front desk.
- **Step 2**: Complete the application, attaching:
    - Copy of valid Business Registration Certificate (BR).
    - Copy of Business License.
    - Estimated breakdown of daily waste type and volume (organic lbs/day vs. recyclable lbs/day).
- **Step 3**: The designated **County Public Health Inspector** or **Waste Management Supervisor** will conduct a mandatory site visit to physically verify the waste storage area, segregation compliance, and volume.
- **Step 4**: The Inspector/Supervisor will recommend a Volume Tier (A, B, C, or Custom) and the corresponding monthly fee. The applicant receives the fee assessment letter.
- **Step 5**: Pay the first month's fee or register for billing at the **Payment Counter**.
- **Step 6**: Upon payment verification, the Cleansing Division issues an official **Waste Collection Authorization Card and Sticker** (displayed on the bin). The premises is added to the commercial collection route.

## 5. Fees & Penalties
- **Household Collection Charge**: Free for standard compliant segregated waste bins. (Service cost is absorbed within the General Assessment/Property Tax Rates.)
- **Commercial High-Volume Collection Monthly Fee (Tier System)**:
    - *Tier A (Low Volume: ~20-50 lbs/day)*: $60.00 - $140.00 per month.
    - *Tier B (Medium Volume: ~51-150 lbs/day)*: $160.00 - $320.00 per month.
    - *Tier C (High Volume / Hotels / Factories: >150 lbs/day)*: $400.00 - $600.00+ per month (assessed case-by-case).
- **Bulk / Garden / Special Removal Fee**:
    - Small Truck Load (single axle): $100.00.
    - Large Truck/Roll-off Load: $180.00 - $240.00.
- **Penalties & Fines**:
    - **Open Dumping / Roadside Littering / Open Burning**: Fine of **$200.00 to $2,000.00**, which may be compounded by the Environmental Officer/County Executive Officer, or prosecuted under the California Integrated Waste Management Act in County Superior Court.
    - **Non-Segregation of Waste**: Refusal of service, followed by a mandatory compounding fee starting from $75.00.
    - **Unlawful Disposal of Commercial Waste using Household Bins**: Immediate confiscation and fine of $600.00.
