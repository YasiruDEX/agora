# Environmental Protection License (EPL) - Health & Environment Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: Regulatory permit issued to ensure that commercial and industrial activities operate within the prescribed state environmental standards. This license controls the emission of pollutants, disposal of waste (solid, liquid, gaseous), and noise levels to prevent environmental degradation and public nuisance. For Riverside County, this pertains strictly to **Category 'C'** prescribed activities as delegated by the California Environmental Protection Agency (CalEPA).
- **Governing Laws**:
    - California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), as amended.
    - California Code of Regulations, Title 22, and the prevailing bulletin listing Prescribed Activities and categorization.
    - Relevant Riverside County By-laws for trade waste and nuisance.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- The enterprise must be classified as a **Low-Polluting, Category 'C' Industry** as delegated by CalEPA to Riverside County. Common eligible activities include:
    - Rice/grain mills (capacity ≤ specific thresholds).
    - Saw mills and carpentry workshops.
    - Automobile repair / Vehicle Service Stations (excluding full-scale spray painting booths).
    - Small-scale garment/textile factories (without wet processing/dyeing).
    - Guest houses, restaurants, and hotels (based on room count/capacity).
    - Bakeries and confectioneries (non-automated).
    - Printing presses and photocopy centers (small-scale).
- The business must be located within the administrative limits of Riverside County.
- The activity must not fall under the exclusive purview of CalEPA (Category A or B).

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
- **Duly completed EPL Application Form**: Standard Form A (Category 'C').
- **Business Registration Certificate (BR)**: Issued by Riverside County DSS or the California Secretary of State.
- **Driver's License or State ID number**: Copy of the proprietor's/directors' identification.
- **Proof of Land/Property Ownership**:
    - Certified copy of the Deed of Transfer, OR
    - Valid Notarized Rent/Lease Agreement.
- **Planning & Building Approvals**:
    - Approved Building Plan from Riverside County.
    - Certificate of Conformity (COC) for the building structure.
- **Operational Flow & Machinery Layout**:
    - Detailed Process Flow Diagram showing step-by-step manufacturing or service activity.
    - Floor plan indicating the placement of machinery.
- **Environmental Control Plan**: Detailed report outlining:
    - Solid Waste disposal methodology (collection, storage, site clearance).
    - Wastewater/Effluent treatment design (soakage pits, grease traps, septic systems).
    - Acoustic/Noise control measures (silencers, acoustic enclosures, vibration dampeners).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
1.  **Application Acquisition & Preparation**:
    - Visit the Riverside County **Health & Environment Division** office on Magnolia Ave and request the **Form A (EPL Application for Category C)** from the Environmental Officer.
    - Attach all mandatory documents listed in Section 3.
2.  **Submission & Payment of Inspection Fee**:
    - Submit the completed file to the dedicated counter.
    - Pay the non-refundable **Inspection Fee** at the **Payment Counter**. Retain the receipt (receipt number must be referenced in all future correspondence).
3.  **Joint Technical Inspection**:
    - A site inspection is scheduled and conducted jointly by the **Riverside County Environmental Officer** and the **County Public Health Inspector**.
    - The team verifies physical layout against submitted plans and checks for environmental hazards.
    - An Inspection Report (Form B) is generated, noting compliance levels.
4.  **Compliance Rectification (Conditional)**:
    - If deficiencies are noted (e.g., open waste burning, poor ventilation, noise pollution, lack of grease trap), a written **Notice of Deficiency** is served to the applicant.
    - The applicant must implement corrective measures (e.g., install silencers, construct dedicated septic tanks, install filters) within a stipulated period (usually 14-21 days).
5.  **Approval, Payment & Issuance**:
    - A follow-up compliance inspection is conducted.
    - The recommendation report is placed before the **Environmental Committee** or the Standing Committee delegated with such powers.
    - Upon final approval, the applicant is directed to pay the annual **License Fee** at the Payment Counter.
    - The **Environmental Protection License (EPL)** certificate is issued. The license is valid for a minimum of **1 year**, up to a maximum of **3 years**, and must be renewed annually.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Inspection Fee**: $75.00 - $150.00 (Non-refundable; varies based on scale of operation).
- **License Fee (Annual)**:
    - Based on the total capital investment of the enterprise.
    - Investment < $50,000: ~$150.00.
    - Investment $50,000 - $250,000: ~$250.00 - $400.00.
    - Investment > $250,000: $500.00+.
    *Note: This is separate from the Business License/Property Assessment fees paid to the County Revenue Division.*
- **Service Timeline (SLA)**: **21 to 30 Working Days** from the date of submission of a fully completed application with no pending deficiencies.
