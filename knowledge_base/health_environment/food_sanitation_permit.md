# Food Sanitation Permit - Health & Environment Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** A mandatory health registration and sanitation permit required for all commercial establishments involved in the manufacturing, preparation, storage, packing, transport, or sale of food intended for public consumption. This includes restaurants, bakeries, school cafeterias, catering services, food processing plants, supermarkets, and grocery stores. Operation without this permit is a punishable offense.
- **Governing Laws & Regulations:**
    - California Retail Food Code (Health & Safety Code) and the Food Hygiene Regulations issued thereunder.
    - Food Establishment Registration Regulations.
    - Riverside County Ordinance (Section 227-229) for county by-laws pertaining to nuisances and sanitary conveniences.
    - Riverside County (Standard) Sanitation By-laws.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Structural & Facility Compliance:** The premises must satisfy the minimum structural, ventilation, lighting, water supply, drainage, and solid/liquid waste disposal standards as determined by the County Health Officer and County Public Health Inspector. Floors, walls, and food contact surfaces must be impervious and washable.
- **Food Handler Compliance:** Every employee who handles food directly or indirectly must undergo the prescribed medical screening and possess a valid Food Handler's Medical Clearance Card issued by the County Health Officer or a designated Riverside County Medical Center physician.
- **Legal Clearance:** The establishment must hold a valid Business License for the current year issued by Riverside County. If the premises is newly constructed, the approved Building Plan and Certificate of Conformity (COC) must be presented. The use of the premises must conform to the County Development Plan/zoning regulations.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents are mandatory for application submission:
- Duly completed **Food Establishment Registration Application Form** (available from the County Health Officer's office/Health Division).
- Certified copy of the proprietor's/owner's **Driver's License or State ID number**.
- Copy of **Business Name Registration Certificate (Form 48/BR Certificate)** from Riverside County DSS.
- Copy of the **Deed of Transfer**, Registered Lease Agreement, or legally valid Rent Agreement for the premises.
- **Original Medical Examination Reports and Health Clearance Cards** for all food handlers (valid for one year from date of issue).
- **Water Quality Test Report** (Bacteriological: Coliform/E.coli; Chemical: Hardness, Heavy Metals) from an accredited laboratory or the Riverside County Water District, dated within the last three months.
- **Layout Plan of the premises**, clearly indicating the kitchen, storage, wash-up areas, and the grease trap/liquid waste management system.
- Copy of **Pest Control Service Agreement/Contract** (mandatory for restaurants, bakeries, hotels, and food processing units; must specify quarterly treatment frequency).
- **Certificate of Conformity (COC)** or approved Building Plan (for newly constructed or renovated food premises).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Collection & Guidance**
    Obtain the prescribed application form and the hygiene specifications checklist from the Health & Sanitation Division of Riverside County or the County Health Officer's office.

- **Step 2: Document Submission**
    Submit the completed application form along with all mandatory attachments to the Health & Sanitation Section/County Public Health Inspector office. The applicant must file a specific inspection date request with the inspector's schedule.

- **Step 3: Physical Inspection & Code Audit**
    The assigned County Public Health Inspector will conduct an unannounced or scheduled physical inspection of the premises. The inspector verifies: structural suitability, adequacy of water supply, food storage temperatures, cross-contamination prevention, personal hygiene of handlers, ventilation, and liquid waste/grease trap functionality.

- **Step 4: Compliance Rectification**
    If defects (e.g., absence of fly screens, missing trap, improper drainage) are observed, the inspector issues a "Notice of Defects and Non-Compliance" specifying a compliance deadline (typically 7-14 days). A re-inspection is scheduled only after the applicant rectifies these deficiencies.

- **Step 5: County Health Officer Endorsement & Approval**
    Upon satisfactory compliance, the inspector submits the inspection report with a recommendation to the County Health Officer. The County Health Officer countersigns the application, granting sanitary clearance.

- **Step 6: Payment & Permit Issuance**
    The applicant proceeds to the Riverside County Payment Counter to pay the prescribed Registration/Permit Fee. The Food Sanitation Permit is issued, bearing a unique registration number, permit validity period, and inspection frequency condition. This permit must be prominently displayed on the premises.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application & Inspection Fee:** $50 to $100 (non-refundable inspection/processing fee, paid at submission).
- **Annual Registration/Permit Fee (Assessment-based):**
    - Small-scale (e.g., coffee kiosk, grocery): $100 - $200.
    - Medium-scale (e.g., standard restaurant, bakery): $200 - $350.
    - Large-scale (e.g., hotel, catering hall, processing plant): $350 - $500.
    *(Fees are determined by Riverside County's Annual Assessment and Fee Schedule.)*
- **Food Handler Medical Screening Fee:** As per prevailing Riverside County Medical Center consultation/clinical charges per worker.
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:** 10 to 21 working days from submission of the completed application, contingent upon passing the initial inspection without major structural non-compliance.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Public Health & Sanitation Division (Office of the County Health Officer).
- **Key Officers:** County Public Health Inspector / County Health Officer.
- **Contact Email:** health@riversidecounty.gov
- **Office Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (excluding federal and county holidays).
- **Escalation Grievance:** If permit issuance is unreasonably delayed despite passing inspection, or in cases of alleged harassment by the inspector, a written complaint may be escalated to the **Food Advisory Committee** (through the County Health Officer) or the **Riverside County Executive Officer**.
