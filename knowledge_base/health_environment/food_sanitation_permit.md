# Food Sanitation Permit - Health & Environment Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** A mandatory health registration and sanitation permit required for all commercial establishments involved in the manufacturing, preparation, storage, packing, transport, or sale of food intended for public consumption. This includes restaurants, bakeries, school canteens, catering services, food processing plants, supermarkets, and grocery stores. Operation without this permit is a punishable offense.
- **Governing Laws & Regulations:**
    - Food Act No. 26 of 1980 and the Food (Hygiene) Regulations issued thereunder.
    - Food Establishment Registration Regulations.
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance (Section 227-229) / Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 (Section 129-131) for local authority by-laws pertaining to nuisances and sanitary conveniences.
    - Local Authority (Standard) Sanitation By-laws.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Structural & Facility Compliance:** The premises must satisfy the minimum structural, ventilation, lighting, water supply, drainage, and solid/liquid waste disposal standards as determined by the Medical Officer of Health (MOH) and Public Health Inspector (PHI). Floors, walls, and food contact surfaces must be impervious and washable.
- **Food Handler Compliance:** Every employee who handles food directly or indirectly must undergo the prescribed medical screening and possess a valid Food Handler’s Medical Clearance Card issued by the MOH or a designated Government Medical Officer.
- **Legal Clearance:** The establishment must hold a valid Trade License for the current year issued by the local authority. If the premises is newly constructed, the approved Building Plan and Certificate of Conformity (COC) must be presented. The use of the premises must conform to the Development Plan/UDA zoning regulations.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents are mandatory for application submission:
- Duly completed **Food Establishment Registration Application Form** (available from the MOH office/Health Division).
- Certified copy of the proprietor's/owner's **National Identity Card (NIC)**.
- Copy of **Business Name Registration Certificate (Form 48/BR Certificate)** from the Divisional Secretariat.
- Copy of the **Deed of Transfer**, Registered Lease Agreement, or legally valid Rent Agreement for the premises.
- **Original Medical Examination Reports and Health Clearance Cards** for all food handlers (valid for one year from date of issue).
- **Water Quality Test Report** (Bacteriological: Coliform/E.coli; Chemical: Hardness, Heavy Metals) from an accredited laboratory or the National Water Supply & Drainage Board (NWSDB), dated within the last three months.
- **Layout Plan of the premises**, clearly indicating the kitchen, storage, wash-up areas, and the grease trap/liquid waste management system.
- Copy of **Pest Control Service Agreement/Contract** (mandatory for restaurants, bakeries, hotels, and food processing units; must specify quarterly treatment frequency).
- **Certificate of Conformity (COC)** or approved Building Plan (for newly constructed or renovated food premises).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Collection & Guidance**
    Obtain the prescribed application form and the hygiene specifications checklist from the Health & Sanitation Division of the Local Authority or the area Medical Officer of Health (MOH) office.

- **Step 2: Document Submission**
    Submit the completed application form along with all mandatory attachments to the Health & Sanitation Section/Area PHI office. The applicant must file a specific inspection date request with the PHI's diary.

- **Step 3: Physical Inspection & Code Audit**
    The area Public Health Inspector (PHI) will conduct an unannounced or scheduled physical inspection of the premises. The PHI verifies: structural suitability, adequacy of water supply, food storage temperatures, cross-contamination prevention, personal hygiene of handlers, ventilation, and liquid waste/grease trap functionality.

- **Step 4: Compliance Rectification**
    If defects (e.g., absence of fly screens, missing trap, improper drainage) are observed, the PHI issues a "Notice of Defects and Non-Compliance" specifying a compliance deadline (typically 7-14 days). A re-inspection is scheduled only after the applicant rectifies these deficiencies.

- **Step 5: MOH Endorsement & Approval**
    Upon satisfactory compliance, the PHI submits the inspection report with a recommendation to the Medical Officer of Health (MOH). The MOH countersigns the application, granting sanitary clearance.

- **Step 6: Payment & Permit Issuance**
    The applicant proceeds to the Local Authority Shroff Counter to pay the prescribed Registration/Permit Fee. The Food Sanitation Permit is issued, bearing a unique registration number, permit validity period, and inspection frequency condition. This permit must be prominently displayed on the premises.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application & Inspection Fee:** LKR 500 to LKR 1,000 (non-refundable inspection/processing fee, paid at submission).
- **Annual Registration/Permit Fee (Assessment-based):**
    - Small-scale (e.g., tea kiosk, grocery): LKR 1,500 - LKR 3,000.
    - Medium-scale (e.g., standard restaurant, bakery): LKR 3,000 - LKR 5,000.
    - Large-scale (e.g., hotel, catering hall, processing plant): LKR 5,000 - LKR 7,500.
    *(Fees are determined by the Local Authority’s Annual Assessment and Varipanam Schedule.)*
- **Food Handler Medical Screening Fee:** As per prevailing Government Hospital consultation/clinical charges per worker.
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:** 10 to 21 working days from submission of the completed application, contingent upon passing the initial PHI inspection without major structural non-compliance.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Public Health & Sanitation Division (Office of the Medical Officer of Health).
- **Key Officers:** Area Public Health Inspector (PHI) / Medical Officer of Health (MOH).
- **Contact Email:** health@council.gov.lk
- **Office Hours:** Weekdays (Monday to Friday), 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Closed on Public and Municipal holidays.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If permit issuance is unreasonably delayed despite passing inspection, or in cases of alleged harassment by the PHI, a written complaint may be escalated to the **Food Advisory Committee** (through the MOH) or the **Municipal Commissioner / Secretary of the Pradeshiya Sabha**.
