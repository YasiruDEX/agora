# Street Line & Building Line Certificate - Permits & Licensing Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** An official certificate issued by the local authority declaring the exact position of a specified land parcel or property relative to the declared street line (road reservation boundary) and building line (setback limit). This certificate is a mandatory prerequisite for verifying whether any portion of the land or existing structures are affected by planned road widening schemes, future road reservations, or building control limits. It is commonly required for land transactions, mortgage/bank loan applications, and as a pre-condition for submission of building plans.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Housing and Town Improvement Ordinance (Chapter 268).
    - Urban Development Authority (UDA) Act No. 41 of 1978 (UDA declared planning zones).
    - Thoroughfares Act (Chapter 286) and Road Development Authority (RDA) road reservation circulars.
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance (Section 227) / Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 (Section 129).
    - Local Authority Planning & Building Regulations and applicable Development Plans.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who Can Apply:**
    - Registered owner(s) of the land/property.
    - Prospective purchasers holding a valid Power of Attorney or a signed Sale & Purchase Agreement from the owner.
    - Licensed Surveyors acting on behalf of the landowner with a letter of authorization.
    - Banks and financial institutions acting on behalf of a borrower/mortgagor (with written consent of the property owner).
    - Legal representatives/attorneys-at-law holding a valid proxy or authorization.
- **Land Requirements:** The land parcel must be situated within the administrative limits of the issuing local authority.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents are mandatory for processing:
- Duly completed **Street Line Certificate Application Form** (obtained from Planning Division).
- Certified copy of the **Licensed Surveyor's Survey Plan** (recent, preferably dated within the last five years, clearly depicting boundaries, extents, and road reservations if any).
- Certified copy of the registered **Title Deed of Transfer** or **Land Registry Extract** (Folio extract). A 30-year ownership chain/title search report may be requested for disputed or fragmented lands.
- Clear copy of the applicant's **National Identity Card (NIC)** (and original for verification).
- **Uncertified Tracing/Location Sketch** showing the property in relation to the main access road, immediate neighboring properties, and significant landmarks.
- Copy of the latest **Assessment Rate Bill / Varipanam Receipt** or the municipal Assessment Account Number (if the property is already assessed by the local authority).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition**
    Collect the prescribed application form from the Planning & Building Control Division (Street Line Unit) or download it from the local authority's official web portal.

- **Step 2: Document Lodgment**
    Submit the completed application form together with the full set of mandatory attachments over the Planning Division front counter. The application must be recorded in the Street Line Register.

- **Step 3: Fee Payment**
    Proceed to the Shroff Counter to pay the non-refundable application processing fee and the certificate preparation fee, and obtain the fiscal receipt. Attach a copy of the receipt to the file.

- **Step 4: Field Inspection & Geometric Mapping**
    The assigned Technical Officer (TO) or Municipal Draftsman will conduct a physical field inspection. The officer verifies the physical boundaries against the Licensed Surveyor’s plan, measures the existing road centerline/frontage distance, and superimposes the survey plan onto the municipal road widening schemes, Development Plan reservations, and RDA/provincial thoroughfare schedules.

- **Step 5: Technical Verification & Endorsement**
    The field report and the superimposed tracing are scrutinized and endorsed by the Planning Officer (for planning/zoning adherence) and the Municipal Engineer (for geometric/road reservation technical accuracy). Any encroachment into the street line or building line is explicitly noted in the report.

- **Step 6: Final Authorization & Certificate Generation**
    The verified report is forwarded for final administrative approval. The **Municipal Commissioner** or **Secretary of the Pradeshiya Sabha** signs the official Street Line & Building Line Certificate, affixing the council seal.

- **Step 7: Certificate Collection**
    The applicant or an authorized representative collects the signed certificate from the Planning Counter upon presentation of the original fiscal receipt and identification. Alternatively, the certificate may be dispatched via Registered Post to the mailing address provided in the application.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Form Fee:** LKR 250 to LKR 500 (non-refundable).
- **Certificate Processing Fee:** Ranges from LKR 1,500 to LKR 5,000. The exact fee is calculated based on the length of the road frontage (linear feet/meters), the land extent, and the estimated land valuation band. A surcharge may apply for urgent processing.
- **Standard SLA / Timeline:** 7 to 14 working days from the date of submission of the complete application and fiscal receipt. Delays may occur if the road reservation plan requires clarification from the UDA or RDA.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Planning & Building Control Division (Street Line Unit).
- **Key Officers:** Technical Officer (TO) / Planning Officer / Municipal Engineer.
- **Contact Email:** streetlines@council.gov.lk
- **Office Hours:** Weekdays (Monday to Friday), 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Closed on Public and Municipal holidays.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If the certificate is not issued within the SLA or if the declared reservations appear inconsistent with the ground reality, a written appeal for re-survey may be lodged with the **Municipal Commissioner / Pradeshiya Sabha Secretary**, citing the survey plan number and application receipt details.
