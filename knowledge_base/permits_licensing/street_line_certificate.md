# Street Line & Building Line Certificate - Permits & Licensing Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** An official certificate issued by Riverside County declaring the exact position of a specified land parcel or property relative to the declared street line (road right-of-way boundary) and building line (setback limit). This certificate is a mandatory prerequisite for verifying whether any portion of the land or existing structures are affected by planned road widening schemes, future road reservations, or building control limits. It is commonly required for land transactions, mortgage/bank loan applications, and as a pre-condition for submission of building plans.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Riverside County Building Code (Title 15).
    - Riverside County Planning & Development Department Enabling Ordinance (County declared planning zones).
    - California Streets and Highways Code and Riverside County Transportation Department road reservation circulars.
    - Riverside County Charter (Section 227) / County Land Use Ordinance (Section 129).
    - County Planning & Building Regulations and applicable General Plans.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who Can Apply:**
    - Registered owner(s) of the land/property.
    - Prospective purchasers holding a valid Power of Attorney or a signed Purchase & Sale Agreement from the owner.
    - Licensed Land Surveyors acting on behalf of the landowner with a letter of authorization.
    - Banks and financial institutions acting on behalf of a borrower/mortgagor (with written consent of the property owner).
    - Legal representatives/attorneys holding a valid proxy or authorization.
- **Land Requirements:** The land parcel must be situated within the administrative limits of Riverside County.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents are mandatory for processing:
- Duly completed **Street Line Certificate Application Form** (obtained from the Planning & Development Department).
- Certified copy of the **Licensed Land Surveyor's Survey Plan** (recent, preferably dated within the last five years, clearly depicting boundaries, extents, and road reservations if any).
- Certified copy of the recorded **Grant Deed** or **County Recorder's Office Extract** (parcel extract). A 30-year ownership chain/title search report may be requested for disputed or fragmented lands.
- Clear copy of the applicant's **Driver's License or State ID number** (and original for verification).
- **Uncertified Tracing/Location Sketch** showing the property in relation to the main access road, immediate neighboring properties, and significant landmarks.
- Copy of the latest **Property Tax Bill** or the County Assessor's Parcel Number (APN) (if the property is already assessed by the County).

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition**
    Collect the prescribed application form from the Building & Safety Division (Street Line Unit) or download it from the County's official web portal.

- **Step 2: Document Lodgment**
    Submit the completed application form together with the full set of mandatory attachments over the Planning & Development Department front counter. The application must be recorded in the Street Line Register.

- **Step 3: Fee Payment**
    Proceed to the County Cashier's Office to pay the non-refundable application processing fee and the certificate preparation fee, and obtain the fiscal receipt. Attach a copy of the receipt to the file.

- **Step 4: Field Inspection & Geometric Mapping**
    The assigned Building Inspector or County Draftsman will conduct a physical field inspection. The officer verifies the physical boundaries against the Licensed Land Surveyor's plan, measures the existing road centerline/frontage distance, and superimposes the survey plan onto the County's road widening schemes, General Plan reservations, and Transportation Department right-of-way schedules.

- **Step 5: Technical Verification & Endorsement**
    The field report and the superimposed tracing are scrutinized and endorsed by the Planning Officer (for planning/zoning adherence) and the County Building Official (for geometric/road reservation technical accuracy). Any encroachment into the street line or building line is explicitly noted in the report.

- **Step 6: Final Authorization & Certificate Generation**
    The verified report is forwarded for final administrative approval. The **Riverside County Executive Officer** or designee signs the official Street Line & Building Line Certificate, affixing the County seal.

- **Step 7: Certificate Collection**
    The applicant or an authorized representative collects the signed certificate from the Planning counter upon presentation of the original fiscal receipt and identification. Alternatively, the certificate may be dispatched via certified mail to the mailing address provided in the application.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Form Fee:** $35 to $75 (non-refundable).
- **Certificate Processing Fee:** Ranges from $150 to $500. The exact fee is calculated based on the length of the road frontage (linear feet), the land extent, and the estimated land valuation band. A surcharge may apply for urgent processing.
- **Standard SLA / Timeline:** 7 to 14 working days from the date of submission of the complete application and fiscal receipt. Delays may occur if the road reservation plan requires clarification from the Planning & Development Department or Transportation Department.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Building & Safety Division, Riverside County Planning & Development Department (Street Line Unit).
- **Key Officers:** Building Inspector / Planning Officer / County Building Official.
- **Contact Email:** streetlines@riversidecounty.gov
- **Office Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (excluding federal holidays).
- **Escalation Grievance:** If the certificate is not issued within the SLA or if the declared reservations appear inconsistent with the ground reality, a written appeal for re-survey may be lodged with the **Riverside County Executive Officer**, citing the survey plan number and application receipt details.
