# Building Plan Approval - Permits & Licensing Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** Official statutory approval required before initiating any construction, extension, or alteration of residential, commercial, or industrial buildings within the Local Authority area. No person shall commence building operations without a valid Development Permit and approved Building Plan.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
  - Urban Development Authority (UDA) Act No. 41 of 1978 (as amended) – UDA Zoning Regulations & Planning Standards
  - Housing and Town Improvement Ordinance (Chapter 268)
  - Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252) / Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255) / Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987
  - Local Authority Building By-laws applicable to the specific council
  - National Environmental Act No. 47 of 1980 (as amended) – for prescribed activities requiring EPL/EPL
  - Sri Lanka Land Reclamation and Development Corporation Act – where applicable for low-lying lands
  - Fire Service Ordinance – for commercial and high-rise structures
  - Coast Conservation Act – for coastal zone properties (if applicable)

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who Can Apply:**
  - Property owner(s) possessing valid title to the land
  - Legally authorized Power of Attorney holder with registered Deed of Power of Attorney
  - Lessee with registered Lease Agreement (subject to lessor's consent where required by by-law)

- **Land Requirements:**
  - Land must be situated within the administrative limits of the Local Authority
  - Must possess an approved Survey Plan prepared by a Licensed Surveyor registered with the Surveyor General's Department
  - Minimum road frontage/access as prescribed by local by-laws (typically 20 feet for main roads, 12 feet for sub-roads/private access ways; verify specific council regulations)
  - Land must not be identified as a reservation, retention area, or prohibited zone under UDA Zoning Plan or Local Authority Development Plan
  - Clear ownership title free from unresolved encumbrances or encroachments on public land
  - Setbacks (Building Line, Street Line, rear/side spaces) must conform to UDA Planning & Building Regulations and Local Authority By-laws

## 4. Application Process & Steps

### Step 1: Preliminary Inquiry & Form Collection
- Visit the Planning & Building Control Division of the respective Municipal Council / Urban Council / Pradeshiya Sabha
- Collect the prescribed Building Application Form from the Shroff Counter or Planning Division
- Pay the non-refundable Application Form Fee at the Shroff Counter and obtain receipt
- Consult the Technical Officer (TO) for preliminary guidance on zoning, permissible coverage, setbacks, building height, road access requirements, and any special conditions applicable

### Step 2: Preparation of Plans
- Engage a **Chartered Architect** (registered with Sri Lanka Institute of Architects) or **Registered Draughtsman** to prepare architectural drawings in conformity with:
  - UDA Planning & Building Regulations (coverage ratio, Floor Area Ratio, setbacks, height limits)
  - Local Authority Building By-laws
  - Relevant zoning designation under UDA Zoning Plan
- Engage a **Chartered Structural Engineer** (registered with Institute of Engineers Sri Lanka) for structural design and stability undertaking (mandatory for ≥2 storeys)
- Obtain Street Line and Building Line Certificates from Local Authority/UDA as required
- Ensure all drawings are signed by preparing professionals and landowner

### Step 3: Document Submission
- Compile all required documents as per Checklist (Section 3)
- Submit completed application dossier (3–4 sets of all documents) to the Planning Division
- Pay the **Inspection Fee** (initial scrutiny charge) at the Shroff Counter and obtain official receipt
- Obtain dated acknowledgment receipt/register entry from the Planning Division confirming submission
- Ensure the receiving officer verifies completeness of submission and notes any deficiencies

### Step 4: On-Site Inspection
- **Technical Officer (TO)** conducts physical site inspection to:
  - Verify survey plan boundaries, physical dimensions, and existing site conditions
  - Confirm building lines, street lines, and setback compliance
  - Assess road access width and nature of access
  - Verify position of neighbouring structures and drainage provisions
- **Public Health Inspector (PHI)** inspects for:
  - Sanitary and drainage adequacy
  - Solid waste disposal arrangements
  - Compliance with public health requirements under Housing & Town Improvement Ordinance
- Inspection Report prepared and placed before Planning Committee for consideration

### Step 5: Plan Review & Determination
- Application placed before **Local Authority Planning Committee** at next scheduled sitting
- Planning Committee reviews:
  - TO/PHI inspection reports
  - Compliance with UDA regulations, by-laws, and zoning
  - Architectural and structural adequacy
  - Clearances from external agencies (Fire, CEA, NWSDB etc.)
- Planning Committee may:
  - **Approve** – with or without conditions
  - **Defer** – requesting additional information/modifications
  - **Reject** – with reasons stated in writing
- Written decision communicated to applicant following Committee resolution

### Step 6: Fee Payment & Issuance of Permit
- Upon approval, applicant directed to Shroff Counter to pay:
  - **Development/Permit Fee** (calculated per sq. ft. / sq. metre of floor area)
  - Any applicable Betterment Levy / Infrastructure Development Charge
  - Other council-specific charges (scaffolding, hoarding, street obstruction fee if applicable)
- Present payment receipts to Planning Division
- Receive **Stamped Approved Plan** (all copies retained and returned as per council practice) and **Development Permit** (Form specified by by-law)
- Development Permit must be displayed prominently on site during construction

### Step 7: Commencement Notification
- Prior to commencing construction, notify the Local Authority in writing of intended start date
- Maintain approved plan on-site for inspection by authorised officers at all times during construction

## 5. Fees & Timelines

### Fee Structure

| Fee Type | Rate / Description |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Application Form Fee** | LKR 500 – 1,000 (varies by council) |
| **Inspection/Scrutiny Fee** | LKR 1,000 – 5,000 (depending on building category and size) |
| **Development/Permit Fee** | Calculated per square foot / per square metre of Gross Floor Area; rate varies by: Building use (Residential / Commercial / Industrial), Location (within council classification zones), and Council-specific fee schedules. Typical range: LKR 5.00 – LKR 50.00+ per sq. ft. |
| **Betterment Levy / Infrastructure Charge** | Applicable in designated development areas; calculated as % of estimated construction value or fixed rate per sq. ft. (UDA- or council-prescribed) |
| **Structural Stability Undertaking Fee** | LKR 2,000 – 10,000 (where structural engineer submission required) |
| **Plan Amendment Fee** | LKR 1,000 – 5,000 per resubmission (if modifications requested) |
| **Scaffolding/Hoarding Permit Fee** | LKR 1,000 – 5,000 per annum (if encroaching on road reservation) |

*Note: Fee schedules are revised periodically by each Local Authority. Confirm current rates at the Shroff Counter or Planning Division.*

### Standard Service Timeline

| Stage | Indicative Duration |
|-------|---------------------|
| Document verification & acknowledgment | Same day to 3 working days (if complete) |
| Site Inspection by TO/PHI | Within 7–14 working days of submission |
| Submission to Planning Committee | Next scheduled meeting (committees meet fortnightly or monthly) |
| Decision communication | Within 5–7 working days of Committee decision |
| **Total Standard SLA** | **14–30 working days** from date of complete submission (excluding delays attributable to applicant) |

*Complex applications, multi-storey commercial buildings, or applications requiring external agency clearances may require extended processing time.*
