# Building Plan Approval - Permits & Licensing Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** Official statutory approval required before initiating any construction, extension, or alteration of residential, commercial, or industrial buildings within Riverside County. No person shall commence building operations without a valid Development Permit and approved Building Plan.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
  - Riverside County Planning & Development Department Enabling Ordinance (as amended) – County Zoning Regulations & Planning Standards
  - Riverside County Building Code (based on the California Building Standards Code)
  - Riverside County Charter, Title 15 (Buildings and Construction)
  - County Board of Supervisors Land Use Ordinance
  - California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) – for prescribed activities requiring environmental review
  - Riverside County Flood Control and Water Conservation District Act – where applicable for low-lying or flood-prone lands
  - California Fire Code – for commercial and high-rise structures
  - Riverside County Hillside Development Ordinance – for hillside or slope properties (if applicable)

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who Can Apply:**
  - Property owner(s) possessing valid title to the land
  - Legally authorized Power of Attorney holder with a recorded Power of Attorney document
  - Lessee with a recorded Lease Agreement (subject to lessor's consent where required by ordinance)

- **Land Requirements:**
  - Land must be situated within the unincorporated administrative limits of Riverside County
  - Must possess an approved Survey Plan prepared by a Licensed Land Surveyor registered with the California Board for Professional Engineers, Land Surveyors, and Geologists
  - Minimum road frontage/access as prescribed by County ordinance (typically 20 feet for main roads, 12 feet for sub-roads/private access ways; verify specific district regulations)
  - Land must not be identified as a reservation, retention area, or prohibited zone under the County Zoning Plan or General Plan
  - Clear ownership title free from unresolved encumbrances or encroachments on public land
  - Setbacks (Building Line, Street Line, rear/side spaces) must conform to County Planning & Building Regulations and applicable ordinances

## 4. Application Process & Steps

### Step 1: Preliminary Inquiry & Form Collection
- Visit the Building & Safety Division of the Riverside County Planning & Development Department
- Collect the prescribed Building Application Form from the County Cashier's Office or Planning counter
- Pay the non-refundable Application Form Fee at the County Cashier's Office and obtain receipt
- Consult the Building Inspector for preliminary guidance on zoning, permissible coverage, setbacks, building height, road access requirements, and any special conditions applicable

### Step 2: Preparation of Plans
- Engage a **licensed Architect** (registered with the California Architects Board) or **Registered Designer** to prepare architectural drawings in conformity with:
  - County Planning & Building Regulations (coverage ratio, Floor Area Ratio, setbacks, height limits)
  - Riverside County Building Code
  - Relevant zoning designation under the County Zoning Plan
- Engage a **licensed Structural Engineer** (registered with the California Board for Professional Engineers, Land Surveyors, and Geologists) for structural design and stability undertaking (mandatory for ≥2 storeys)
- Obtain Street Line and Building Line Certificates from the County as required
- Ensure all drawings are signed by preparing professionals and landowner

### Step 3: Document Submission
- Compile all required documents as per Checklist (Section 3)
- Submit completed application dossier (3–4 sets of all documents) to the Building & Safety Division
- Pay the **Plan Review Fee** (initial scrutiny charge) at the County Cashier's Office and obtain official receipt
- Obtain dated acknowledgment receipt/register entry from the Division confirming submission
- Ensure the receiving officer verifies completeness of submission and notes any deficiencies

### Step 4: On-Site Inspection
- **Building Inspector** conducts physical site inspection to:
  - Verify survey plan boundaries, physical dimensions, and existing site conditions
  - Confirm building lines, street lines, and setback compliance
  - Assess road access width and nature of access
  - Verify position of neighbouring structures and drainage provisions
- **Environmental Health Specialist** inspects for:
  - Sanitary and drainage adequacy
  - Solid waste disposal arrangements
  - Compliance with public health requirements under County health regulations
- Inspection Report prepared and placed before the Planning Commission for consideration

### Step 5: Plan Review & Determination
- Application placed before the **County Planning Commission** at the next scheduled hearing
- Planning Commission reviews:
  - Building Inspector/Environmental Health inspection reports
  - Compliance with County regulations, ordinances, and zoning
  - Architectural and structural adequacy
  - Clearances from external agencies (Fire Department, Environmental Health, Water Authority, etc.)
- Planning Commission may:
  - **Approve** – with or without conditions
  - **Defer** – requesting additional information/modifications
  - **Reject** – with reasons stated in writing
- Written decision communicated to applicant following Commission resolution

### Step 6: Fee Payment & Issuance of Permit
- Upon approval, applicant directed to the County Cashier's Office to pay:
  - **Development/Permit Fee** (calculated per square foot of floor area)
  - Any applicable Impact Fee / Infrastructure Development Charge
  - Other County-specific charges (scaffolding, hoarding, street obstruction fee if applicable)
- Present payment receipts to the Building & Safety Division
- Receive **Stamped Approved Plan** (all copies retained and returned as per County practice) and **Development Permit** (Form specified by ordinance)
- Development Permit must be displayed prominently on site during construction

### Step 7: Commencement Notification
- Prior to commencing construction, notify Riverside County in writing of the intended start date
- Maintain approved plan on-site for inspection by authorized officers at all times during construction

## 5. Fees & Timelines

### Fee Structure

| Fee Type | Rate / Description |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Application Form Fee** | $50 – $100 (varies by district) |
| **Plan Review/Scrutiny Fee** | $150 – $750 (depending on building category and size) |
| **Development/Permit Fee** | Calculated per square foot of Gross Floor Area; rate varies by: Building use (Residential / Commercial / Industrial), Location (within County classification zones), and County-specific fee schedules. Typical range: $1.50 – $10.00+ per sq. ft. |
| **Impact Fee / Infrastructure Charge** | Applicable in designated development areas; calculated as a percentage of estimated construction value or a fixed rate per sq. ft. (County-prescribed) |
| **Structural Stability Undertaking Fee** | $300 – $1,500 (where structural engineer submission required) |
| **Plan Amendment Fee** | $150 – $600 per resubmission (if modifications requested) |
| **Scaffolding/Hoarding Permit Fee** | $150 – $500 per annum (if encroaching on road right-of-way) |

*Note: Fee schedules are revised periodically by the County Board of Supervisors. Confirm current rates at the County Cashier's Office or Building & Safety Division.*

### Standard Service Timeline

| Stage | Indicative Duration |
|-------|---------------------|
| Document verification & acknowledgment | Same day to 3 working days (if complete) |
| Site Inspection by Building Inspector/Environmental Health | Within 7–14 working days of submission |
| Submission to Planning Commission | Next scheduled meeting (Commission meets biweekly or monthly) |
| Decision communication | Within 5–7 working days of Commission decision |
| **Total Standard SLA** | **14–30 working days** from date of complete submission (excluding delays attributable to applicant) |

*Complex applications, multi-storey commercial buildings, or applications requiring external agency clearances may require extended processing time.*

| Level | Contact | Grounds for Escalation |
|-------|---------|------------------------|

| **Level 2** | County Building Official / Engineering Supervisor | Technical disputes, delay beyond SLA, interpretation of ordinance provisions |
| **Level 3** | Riverside County Executive Officer | Unresolved administrative delays, formal grievances |
