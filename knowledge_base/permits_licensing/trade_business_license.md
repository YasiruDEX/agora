# Trade & Business License - Permits & Licensing Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** An annual legal license granting permission to operate any commercial, industrial, or service-oriented business within the administrative limits of the Local Authority. The license must be renewed before the commencement of each calendar year. Operating without a valid license constitutes an offense subject to legal action and penalty surcharges.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance No. 29 of 1947 (Sections 233–247) / Urban Councils Ordinance No. 61 of 1939 / Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 (Sections 93–102).
    - National Environmental Act No. 47 of 1980 (as amended by Act No. 56 of 1988).
    - Food Act No. 26 of 1980 and Food (Hygiene) Regulations 2011.
    - By-laws of the respective Local Authority on Trade Licensing and Nuisance Control.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Premises Ownership/Lease:** The applicant must be the legal owner or authorized lessee of the premises. The premises must hold an approved Building Plan and a valid Certificate of Conformity (COC) issued by the Local Authority, or a valid change-of-use permit if the building category differs from the business activity.
- **Health & Environmental Compliance:** The business activity must strictly comply with sanitation guidelines enforced by the Public Health Inspector (PHI) and must not exceed noise, vibration, or air/water pollution thresholds stipulated by the Central Environmental Authority (CEA). No hazardous nuisance to the public or neighboring properties is permitted.
- **Zoning Compliance:** The business must conform to the land-use zoning designated under the Local Authority’s Development Plan and UDA regulations. Business activities in residential zones require a non-residential activity clearance.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
- Completed Trade License Application Form (duly signed by the applicant).
- Copy of Business Name Registration (BR) Certificate issued by the Divisional Secretariat.
- Copy of Applicant’s National Identity Card (NIC) (if a company, NICs of all Directors/Partners).
- Deed of Transfer for the property or a Registered Rent/Lease Agreement (notarized) with consent letter from the property owner.
- Approved Building Plan and Certificate of Conformity (COC) copy (certified by the Municipal Engineer/Pradeshiya Sabha Engineer).
- Environmental Protection License (EPL) from the Central Environmental Authority (CEA) – mandatory for industrial/manufacturing/noisy trades, sawmills, automobile repairs, and large-scale laundries.
- Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Fire Services Department – mandatory for high-risk/hazardous trades (e.g., fuel stations, chemical storage, cinemas, garment factories).
- Food Handling Training Certificates & Water/Food Test Reports – mandatory for eating houses, bakeries, food processing units, canteens, and tea boutiques. Food testing must be done by an approved Public Health Laboratory.
- Grama Niladhari (GN) Certificate of Residence & Character (indicating no public objection to the business).
- Photos of the business premises (exterior and interior) taken within the last 3 months.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1:** Collect the prescribed Application Form from the Revenue & Licensing Division (Shroff Counter) of the Local Authority upon payment of the application form fee.
- **Step 2:** Submit the completed form with all mandatory supporting documents to the Subject Clerk (Licensing Counter). Receive the acknowledgment receipt with the file reference number.
- **Step 3:** A joint premises inspection is conducted by the Public Health Inspector (PHI) and the Revenue Inspector, who verify health, sanitation, structural safety, and measurement particulars. The Fire Officer and CEA officer may be requested for joint inspection depending on business classification.
- **Step 4:** The PHI and Revenue Inspector submit an Inspection Recommendation Report to the Standing Committee on Planning, Environment, and Licensing (Trade Committee) for approval or conditional approval.
- **Step 5:** Upon committee approval, the Revenue Inspector calculates the annual assessment value (Varipanam) and trade license fee. The applicant pays the approved Trade License Fee, taxes, and any surcharges at the Shroff Counter and obtains the official receipt.
- **Step 6:** Collect the signed Trade License Certificate from the Revenue & Licensing Division. The original license (or a certified copy) must be prominently displayed in a glass frame at the business premises for public display and inspector verification.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Form Fee:** LKR 250.00 – 500.00 (non-refundable).
- **Trade License Fee:** Calculated progressively based on the annual rental value (Assessment Rate/Varipanam) of the premises and business classification. Ranges from LKR 1,000.00 for small shops to LKR 50,000.00+ for large-scale industrial establishments.
- **Late Renewal Penalty:** A surcharge of 25% – 100% of the license fee is applied if the license is not renewed before January 31st of the licensing year.
- **Standard SLA / Timeline:** 7 to 14 working days from the date of submission of a complete and accurate application, subject to committee meeting schedules (usually held bi-weekly). EPL applications may extend the timeline by 30 days.
