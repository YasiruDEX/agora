# Trade & Business License - Permits & Licensing Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** An annual legal license granting permission to operate any commercial, industrial, or service-oriented business within the administrative limits of Riverside County. The license must be renewed before the commencement of each calendar year. Operating without a valid license constitutes an offense subject to legal action and penalty surcharges.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Riverside County Business License Ordinance No. 29 (Sections 233–247) / County Land Use Ordinance (Sections 93–102).
    - California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), as amended.
    - California Retail Food Code (CalCode) and County Food Facility Regulations 2011.
    - Ordinances of Riverside County on Business Licensing and Nuisance Control.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Premises Ownership/Lease:** The applicant must be the legal owner or authorized lessee of the premises. The premises must hold an approved Building Plan and a valid Certificate of Occupancy (COO) issued by the County, or a valid change-of-use permit if the building category differs from the business activity.
- **Health & Environmental Compliance:** The business activity must strictly comply with sanitation guidelines enforced by the County Environmental Health Specialist and must not exceed noise, vibration, or air/water pollution thresholds stipulated by the Riverside County Department of Environmental Health. No hazardous nuisance to the public or neighboring properties is permitted.
- **Zoning Compliance:** The business must conform to the land-use zoning designated under the County's General Plan and Riverside County Planning & Development Department regulations. Business activities in residential zones require a non-residential activity clearance.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
- Completed Business License Application Form (duly signed by the applicant).
- Copy of Fictitious Business Name (FBN) Statement filed with the Riverside County Clerk-Recorder.
- Copy of Applicant's Driver's License or State ID number (if a company, IDs of all Directors/Partners).
- Grant Deed for the property or a Registered Rent/Lease Agreement (notarized) with consent letter from the property owner.
- Approved Building Plan and Certificate of Occupancy (COO) copy (certified by the County Building Official).
- Environmental Review Clearance from the Riverside County Department of Environmental Health – mandatory for industrial/manufacturing/noisy trades, auto body shops, automobile repairs, and large-scale laundries.
- Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Riverside County Fire Department – mandatory for high-risk/hazardous trades (e.g., fuel stations, chemical storage, cinemas, garment factories).
- Food Handling Training Certificates & Water/Food Test Reports – mandatory for restaurants, bakeries, food processing units, cafeterias, and coffee shops. Food testing must be done by an approved County Public Health Laboratory.
- Neighborhood Notification / No-Objection Acknowledgment (indicating no public objection to the business).
- Photos of the business premises (exterior and interior) taken within the last 3 months.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1:** Collect the prescribed Application Form from the Revenue & Licensing Division (County Cashier's Office) of Riverside County upon payment of the application form fee.
- **Step 2:** Submit the completed form with all mandatory supporting documents to the Subject Clerk (Licensing Counter). Receive the acknowledgment receipt with the file reference number.
- **Step 3:** A joint premises inspection is conducted by the Environmental Health Specialist and the Tax Compliance Officer, who verify health, sanitation, structural safety, and measurement particulars. The Fire Officer and Environmental Health officer may be requested for joint inspection depending on business classification.
- **Step 4:** The Environmental Health Specialist and Tax Compliance Officer submit an Inspection Recommendation Report to the Standing Committee on Planning, Environment, and Licensing (Business License Committee) for approval or conditional approval.
- **Step 5:** Upon committee approval, the Tax Compliance Officer calculates the annual assessed value and business license fee. The applicant pays the approved Business License Fee, taxes, and any surcharges at the County Cashier's Office and obtains the official receipt.
- **Step 6:** Collect the signed Business License Certificate from the Revenue & Licensing Division. The original license (or a certified copy) must be prominently displayed in a glass frame at the business premises for public display and inspector verification.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application Form Fee:** $35.00 – $75.00 (non-refundable).
- **Business License Fee:** Calculated progressively based on the annual rental value (assessed value) of the premises and business classification. Ranges from $100.00 for small shops to $5,000.00+ for large-scale industrial establishments.
- **Late Renewal Penalty:** A surcharge of 25% – 100% of the license fee is applied if the license is not renewed before January 31st of the licensing year.
- **Standard SLA / Timeline:** 7 to 14 working days from the date of submission of a complete and accurate application, subject to committee meeting schedules (usually held bi-weekly). Environmental Review applications may extend the timeline by 30 days.
