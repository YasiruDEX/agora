# Ownership Registration & Ownership Certificate - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: Official registration of a new property owner in the local authority assessment register and issuance of a Certificate of Ownership. This process updates the ownership records maintained under the rating and taxation powers of the Council, ensuring that future Assessment Rates (Varipanam) demands are issued to the correct legal owner. The Certificate of Ownership serves as prima facie evidence of registration in the Council's fiscal records.
- **Governing Laws**:
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance (Cap. 252), Section 236 (Amendment of Assessment List on transfer of title).
    - Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, Section 153 (corresponding provisions for Pradeshiya Sabhas).
    - Urban Councils Ordinance (Cap. 255), Section 240 (for Urban Council areas, where applicable).
    - Assessment of Rates on Immovable Property (Procedure) Regulations.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- The applicant must be the registered owner of land and/or improvements (buildings) via an executed and registered **Deed of Transfer**, **Deed of Gift**, or **Partition Deed**.
- The title must be absolute and registered at the Provincial or Colombo Land Registry, as applicable.
- **All Assessment Rates (Varipanam) for the property must be fully paid up to the current quarter.** No arrears can be outstanding against the Assessment Number.
- If the property falls within a declared Urban Development Area, conformity with UDA planning and building regulations must be confirmed (non-conformity may result in referral before ownership registration).

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
Applicants must submit one original set and a photocopy set of the following:

1.  **Duly Typed Abstract of Title (AT Form)** : Must be certified by a Notary Public with official seal, covering uninterrupted chain of ownership for the preceding 30 years minimum.
2.  **Notary Public's Covering Letter**: On Notary's letterhead, bearing the official rubber stamp, summarizing the transaction and enclosing the AT Form.
3.  **Certified Copy of the Title Deed**: The primary deed of transfer, gift, or partition, certified by the relevant Land Registry.
4.  **Copy of Approved Survey Plan**: The survey plan attached to the deed, approved by the Surveyor General's Department.
5.  **Computerized Payment Receipt**: Official receipt from the Council's Shroff Counter or online payment gateway, proving Assessment Rates are paid up to the current quarter (no arrears confirmation).
6.  **Devolution of Title Report**: Narrative report covering the past 30 years, prepared and certified by the Notary Public, tracing every succession, transfer, encumbrance, and mutation.
7.  **Copy of Owner's National Identity Card (NIC)** : Clear copy of the valid NIC of the new registered owner(s).
8.  **Certificate of Conformity (COC)** : If applicable, for buildings requiring a Certificate of Conformity under UDA or Local Authority building regulations. (Mandatory for properties with recent constructions or condominium units.)

## 4. Application Process & Steps

### Step 1: Obtain AT Form
Procure the standard **Abstract of Title (AT) Form** physically from the Municipal Assessor's Department counter or download from the Council's official web portal.

### Step 2: Notary Certification
Engage a licensed Notary Public to:
- Perform a **30-year title search** at the Land Registry.
- Extract all relevant prior deeds, encumbrances, and succession events.
- Prepare the **Devolution of Title Report**.
- Fill and certify the **AT Form**, attesting that the abstract matches the Land Registry folios.

### Step 3: Submission of Application Package
Submit the complete set of documents (originals for verification, copies for retention) to the **Municipal Assessor's Department** over the Shroff or registration counter. Obtain a dated acknowledgment receipt with tracking number.

### Step 4: Verification & Title Search
The **Municipal Assessor** or designated Revenue Inspector will:
- Verify all submitted documents for authenticity and completeness.
- Cross-reference with internal Assessment Register and Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).
- Conduct an internal or external title search to confirm the deed matches the Land Registry records and the fiscal survey number (Assessment Number).

### Step 5: Mutation & Register Update
Upon successful verification:
- The name of the new owner is formally **mutated (updated)** in the Council's Assessment Register.
- The old owner's name is struck off.
- A new/updated Demand Notice for the next rating period may be generated.

### Step 6: Issuance of Certificate of Ownership
- The Certificate of Ownership is issued under the seal of the Municipal Assessor.
- The certificate confirms the current registered owner on the Council's fiscal records and the Assessment Number.
- **Delivery**: Sent to the applicant via **Registered Post** to the address provided, or handed over physically upon presentation of the original acknowledgment receipt and NIC at the Assessor's Department counter.

## 5. Fees & Timelines

| Fee Item | Amount (LKR) | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Application & Registration Fee** | 1,000.00 – 3,000.00 | Varies based on annual assessed value (varipanam bracket) of the property. Fees published in Council Gazette. |
| **Certificate Issuance Fee** | 500.00 – 1,000.00 | If charged separately from registration. |
| **Late Mutation Penalty** | Variable | Applicable if ownership change is not notified within the statutory period (typically 3 months from deed execution). |

- **Processing Service Level Agreement (SLA)** : **14 to 30 working days** from the date of submission of a complete, error-free application with no title irregularities.
