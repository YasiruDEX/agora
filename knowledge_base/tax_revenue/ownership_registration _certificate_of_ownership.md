# Ownership Registration & Ownership Certificate - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: Official registration of a new property owner in the Riverside County assessment roll and issuance of a Certificate of Ownership. This process updates the ownership records maintained under the assessment and taxation powers of the County, ensuring that future Assessment Rate (Property Tax) demands are issued to the correct legal owner. The Certificate of Ownership serves as prima facie evidence of registration in the County's fiscal records.
- **Governing Laws**:
    - Riverside County Charter, Section 236 (Amendment of Assessment Roll on transfer of title).
    - California Revenue and Taxation Code, Section 153 (corresponding assessment provisions).
    - Riverside County Land Use Ordinance, Section 240 (for unincorporated areas, where applicable).
    - Assessment of Rates on Immovable Property (Procedure) Regulations.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- The applicant must be the registered owner of land and/or improvements (buildings) via an executed and recorded **Grant Deed**, **Deed of Gift**, or **Partition Deed**.
- The title must be absolute and recorded at the Riverside County Recorder's Office, as applicable.
- **All Assessment Rates (Property Taxes) for the property must be fully paid up to the current quarter.** No arrears can be outstanding against the Assessor's Parcel Number.
- If the property falls within a declared Planning & Development Area, conformity with County planning and building regulations must be confirmed (non-conformity may result in referral before ownership registration).

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
Applicants must submit one original set and a photocopy set of the following:

1.  **Duly Typed Abstract of Title (AT Form)** : Must be certified by a Notary Public with official seal, covering uninterrupted chain of ownership for the preceding 30 years minimum.
2.  **Notary Public's Covering Letter**: On Notary's letterhead, bearing the official notary stamp, summarizing the transaction and enclosing the AT Form.
3.  **Certified Copy of the Title Deed**: The primary grant deed, gift deed, or partition deed, certified by the Riverside County Recorder's Office.
4.  **Copy of Approved Survey Plan**: The survey plan attached to the deed, approved by the County Surveyor's Office.
5.  **Computerized Payment Receipt**: Official receipt from the County Cashier's Office or online payment gateway, proving Assessment Rates are paid up to the current quarter (no arrears confirmation).
6.  **Devolution of Title Report**: Narrative report covering the past 30 years, prepared and certified by the Notary Public, tracing every succession, transfer, encumbrance, and mutation.
7.  **Copy of Owner's Driver's License or State ID number** : Clear copy of the valid ID of the new registered owner(s).
8.  **Certificate of Occupancy (COO)** : If applicable, for buildings requiring a Certificate of Occupancy under County building regulations. (Mandatory for properties with recent constructions or condominium units.)

## 4. Application Process & Steps

### Step 1: Obtain AT Form
Procure the standard **Abstract of Title (AT) Form** physically from the Riverside County Assessor's Office counter or download from the County's official web portal.

### Step 2: Notary Certification
Engage a licensed Notary Public to:
- Perform a **30-year title search** at the Riverside County Recorder's Office.
- Extract all relevant prior deeds, encumbrances, and succession events.
- Prepare the **Devolution of Title Report**.
- Fill and certify the **AT Form**, attesting that the abstract matches the Recorder's Office records.

### Step 3: Submission of Application Package
Submit the complete set of documents (originals for verification, copies for retention) to the **Riverside County Assessor's Office** over the Cashier's or registration counter. Obtain a dated acknowledgment receipt with tracking number.

### Step 4: Verification & Title Search
The **County Assessor** or designated Tax Compliance Officer will:
- Verify all submitted documents for authenticity and completeness.
- Cross-reference with internal Assessment Roll and Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).
- Conduct an internal or external title search to confirm the deed matches the Recorder's Office records and the fiscal parcel number (Assessor's Parcel Number).

### Step 5: Mutation & Roll Update
Upon successful verification:
- The name of the new owner is formally **mutated (updated)** in the County's Assessment Roll.
- The old owner's name is struck off.
- A new/updated Demand Notice for the next rating period may be generated.

### Step 6: Issuance of Certificate of Ownership
- The Certificate of Ownership is issued under the seal of the County Assessor.
- The certificate confirms the current registered owner on the County's fiscal records and the Assessor's Parcel Number.
- **Delivery**: Sent to the applicant via **certified mail** to the address provided, or handed over physically upon presentation of the original acknowledgment receipt and ID at the Assessor's Office counter.

## 5. Fees & Timelines

| Fee Item | Amount (USD) | Remarks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Application & Registration Fee** | $150.00 – $450.00 | Varies based on annual assessed value bracket of the property. Fees published in the County Register. |
| **Certificate Issuance Fee** | $75.00 – $150.00 | If charged separately from registration. |
| **Late Mutation Penalty** | Variable | Applicable if ownership change is not notified within the statutory period (typically 3 months from deed execution). |

- **Processing Service Level Agreement (SLA)** : **14 to 30 working days** from the date of submission of a complete, error-free application with no title irregularities.
