# Property Clearance Certificate - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** An official legal certificate issued by the local authority confirming that a specified property has fully settled all outstanding municipal assessment rates (Varipanam), service charges, trade taxes (if applicable), and any other statutory dues levied by the council up to the declared date of clearance. This certificate serves as irrefutable proof of financial good standing and is an essential prerequisite for property sales, title transfers, mortgage registrations, bank loan processing, and building plan approvals at the Local Authority or Urban Development Authority.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance No. 29 of 1947 (Section 235 – Assessment and Rating provisions).
    - Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255).
    - Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 (Section 139 – Assessment of Rates).
    - Local Authority Financial Regulations and Revenue Recovery By-laws.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who Can Apply:**
    - The registered property owner(s) as per the Assessment Register.
    - A prospective legal buyer holding a valid Power of Attorney or a signed Sale & Purchase Agreement from the owner.
    - Notaries Public or Attorneys-at-Law handling the property conveyance, acting on behalf of the owner with a written request.
    - Banks, leasing companies, or financial institutions processing a mortgage or housing loan application for the subject property, with a formal letter of request.
- **Financial Prerequisite:** All quarterly Assessment Rate charges, arrears, accumulated penalty surcharges, trade license fees (if property is used for business), and any specific improvement charges levied against the property must be fully settled up to the current active billing quarter. An application will not be processed if the property ledger shows an outstanding debit balance.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The applicant must provide the following:
- Duly completed **Property Clearance Certificate Application Form** (obtainable from the Rates Division).
- Clear copy of the applicant's/owner's **National Identity Card (NIC)**. Original must be presented for verification.
- **Most Recent Assessment Rate Bill** (current year/quarter) and the corresponding **Council Computer Receipt/Shroff Receipt** proving payment for the latest billing cycle.
- Certified copy of the registered **Title Deed of Transfer** or **Licensed Surveyor's Survey Plan** specifying the precise Assessment Account Number.
- **Formal Letter of Request** on official letterhead from the Bank, Law Firm, or Leasing Company (mandatory if the certificate is required for mortgage, loan clearance, or property transfer purposes). This letter must clearly state the purpose of the clearance and the property address.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition**
    Collect the Property Clearance Certificate Application Form from the Tax & Revenue Department (Rates & Clearance Counter) or download it from the official local authority web portal.

- **Step 2: Arrears & Ledger Scrutiny**
    Present the Assessment Account Number to the Rates Counter Clerk/Revenue Officer. The officer will pull up the digital property tax ledger to screen for any hidden arrears, default penalty surcharges, unpaid trade license fees, or prior year audit adjustments.

- **Step 3: Settlement of Dues**
    If the ledger reveals any outstanding balance or penalty charges, the applicant must proceed to the Shroff Counter to settle the full amount. The original fiscal receipt (machine-printed) confirming zero balance must be obtained and attached.

- **Step 4: Formal Submission**
    Submit the completed application form together with the fresh payment receipt(s) and all supporting documents to the Rates Section Clearance Desk. The clerk will register the file and issue an acknowledgment slip with a serial number.

- **Step 5: Revenue Verification & Endorsement**
    The Revenue Inspector conducts a final audit of the physical payment records against the Assessment Register to ensure no data entry omissions exist. The Municipal Assessor cross-verifies the Title Deed/Plan details and the property ownership status.

- **Step 6: Certificate Authorization**
    Upon successful verification, the **Municipal Assessor / Chief Revenue Officer** or, for high-value transfers, the **Municipal Commissioner / Secretary of the Pradeshiya Sabha** signs the Property Clearance Certificate, affixing the council seal.

- **Step 7: Certificate Collection**
    The applicant or authorized representative collects the signed certificate from the Rates Counter upon presentation of the acknowledgment slip and original NIC. Alternatively, the certificate may be dispatched via Registered Post.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application & Certificate Fee:**
    - Residential Property: LKR 500 to LKR 1,000.
    - Commercial/Industrial Property: LKR 1,000 to LKR 2,000.
    *(Fees are governed by the Annual Assessment Schedule and Council Fee By-laws.)*
- **Express / Urgent Counter Processing Fee:** Additional LKR 500 (where same-day counter service is operationally available).
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:**
    - **Digital Ledger (Zero Arrears):** Same-day to within 1 working day (over-the-counter service).
    - **Manual Register Verification / Complex Title:** 1 to 3 working days. Delays may occur if the property assessment file requires physical retrieval from the archives.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Tax & Revenue Department (Rates & Clearance Unit).
- **Key Officers:** Revenue Inspector / Municipal Assessor / Chief Revenue Officer.
- **Contact Email:** rates@council.gov.lk
- **Office Hours:** Weekdays (Monday to Friday), 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM. The Shroff Counter closes strictly at 3:00 PM for cash transactions.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If a clearance certificate is unreasonably withheld despite full payment of dues, or if there is a dispute regarding the quantum of arrears, a written appeal must be submitted to the **Municipal Commissioner** or **Secretary of the Pradeshiya Sabha** for an administrative hearing on the property ledger.
