# Property Clearance Certificate - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** An official legal certificate issued by Riverside County confirming that a specified property has fully settled all outstanding assessment rates (property taxes), service charges, business taxes (if applicable), and any other statutory dues levied by the County up to the declared date of clearance. This certificate serves as irrefutable proof of financial good standing and is an essential prerequisite for property sales, title transfers, mortgage registrations, bank loan processing, and building plan approvals at the County or Planning & Development Department.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Riverside County Business License Ordinance No. 29 (Section 235 – Assessment and Rating provisions).
    - Riverside County Land Use Ordinance (Title 15).
    - California Revenue and Taxation Code (Section 139 – Assessment of Rates).
    - County Financial Regulations and Revenue Recovery Ordinances.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who Can Apply:**
    - The registered property owner(s) as per the Assessment Roll.
    - A prospective legal buyer holding a valid Power of Attorney or a signed Purchase & Sale Agreement from the owner.
    - Notaries Public or attorneys handling the property conveyance, acting on behalf of the owner with a written request.
    - Banks, leasing companies, or financial institutions processing a mortgage or housing loan application for the subject property, with a formal letter of request.
- **Financial Prerequisite:** All quarterly Assessment Rate charges, arrears, accumulated penalty surcharges, business license fees (if property is used for business), and any specific improvement charges levied against the property must be fully settled up to the current active billing quarter. An application will not be processed if the property ledger shows an outstanding debit balance.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The applicant must provide the following:
- Duly completed **Property Clearance Certificate Application Form** (obtainable from the Tax Collector's Division).
- Clear copy of the applicant's/owner's **Driver's License or State ID number**. Original must be presented for verification.
- **Most Recent Assessment Rate Bill** (current year/quarter) and the corresponding **County Computer Receipt/Cashier's Receipt** proving payment for the latest billing cycle.
- Certified copy of the recorded **Grant Deed** or **Licensed Land Surveyor's Survey Plan** specifying the precise Assessor's Parcel Number.
- **Formal Letter of Request** on official letterhead from the Bank, Law Firm, or Leasing Company (mandatory if the certificate is required for mortgage, loan clearance, or property transfer purposes). This letter must clearly state the purpose of the clearance and the property address.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition**
    Collect the Property Clearance Certificate Application Form from the Tax & Revenue Department (Rates & Clearance Counter) or download it from the official Riverside County web portal.

- **Step 2: Arrears & Ledger Scrutiny**
    Present the Assessor's Parcel Number to the Tax Collector's Counter Clerk/Revenue Officer. The officer will pull up the digital property tax ledger to screen for any hidden arrears, default penalty surcharges, unpaid business license fees, or prior year audit adjustments.

- **Step 3: Settlement of Dues**
    If the ledger reveals any outstanding balance or penalty charges, the applicant must proceed to the County Cashier's Office to settle the full amount. The original fiscal receipt (machine-printed) confirming zero balance must be obtained and attached.

- **Step 4: Formal Submission**
    Submit the completed application form together with the fresh payment receipt(s) and all supporting documents to the Rates Section Clearance Desk. The clerk will register the file and issue an acknowledgment slip with a serial number.

- **Step 5: Revenue Verification & Endorsement**
    The Tax Compliance Officer conducts a final audit of the physical payment records against the Assessment Roll to ensure no data entry omissions exist. The County Assessor cross-verifies the Grant Deed/Plan details and the property ownership status.

- **Step 6: Certificate Authorization**
    Upon successful verification, the **County Assessor / Chief Revenue Officer** or, for high-value transfers, the **Riverside County Executive Officer**, signs the Property Clearance Certificate, affixing the County seal.

- **Step 7: Certificate Collection**
    The applicant or authorized representative collects the signed certificate from the Tax Collector's Counter upon presentation of the acknowledgment slip and original ID. Alternatively, the certificate may be dispatched via certified mail.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Application & Certificate Fee:**
    - Residential Property: $75 to $150.
    - Commercial/Industrial Property: $150 to $300.
    *(Fees are governed by the Annual Assessment Schedule and County Fee Ordinances.)*
- **Express / Urgent Counter Processing Fee:** Additional $50 (where same-day counter service is operationally available).
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:**
    - **Digital Ledger (Zero Arrears):** Same-day to within 1 working day (over-the-counter service).
    - **Manual Register Verification / Complex Title:** 1 to 3 working days. Delays may occur if the property assessment file requires physical retrieval from the archives.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Tax & Revenue Department (Rates & Clearance Unit).
- **Key Officers:** Tax Compliance Officer / County Assessor / Chief Revenue Officer.
- **Contact Email:** taxcollector@riversidecounty.gov
- **Office Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The County Cashier's Office closes strictly at 4:30 PM for cash transactions.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If a clearance certificate is unreasonably withheld despite full payment of dues, or if there is a dispute regarding the quantum of arrears, a written appeal must be submitted to the **Riverside County Executive Officer** for an administrative hearing on the property ledger.
