# Trade Tax Collection - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** The annual assessment, levy, and collection of trade tax imposed on all commercial, industrial, professional, and financial enterprises operating within the administrative limits of the local authority. This is a distinct statutory tax on the act of conducting business within the council area and is separate from the Trade License (which regulates operational and sanitary compliance). The tax is assessed based on the nature, scale, turnover, and location of the business.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances:**
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance No. 29 of 1947 (Sections 247 and 248 – Trade and Professional Tax provisions).
    - Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255).
    - Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 (Section 143 – Business Tax Assessment).
    - Annual Council Finance Committee Resolutions setting Tax Schedules and Exemption Thresholds.

## 2. Tax Liability & Categorization
- **Who is Liable:** All individuals, registered companies, partnerships, sole proprietorships, and professional service practices that maintain a fixed place of business, office, or commercial outlet within the local authority jurisdiction. This includes banks, insurance companies, private hospitals, guest houses, and consultants. Even if a business holds a Trade License from a different authority, conducting business physically within the council limits triggers tax liability.
- **Tax Classification Tiers:**
    The Revenue Inspector assigns a tier based on the nature and estimated annual turnover of the business:
    - **Tier A (Small Scale):** Micro-enterprises, small retail/kiosk shops, home-based food sellers, hair salons, and sole traders with minimal capital investment.
    - **Tier B (Medium Scale):** Standard restaurants, bakeries, garment shops, pharmacies, hardware stores, automobile repair garages, and service-oriented outlets (communication, IT).
    - **Tier C (Large Scale):** Corporate branch offices, private hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, financial institutions (banks/leasing), large hotels, supermarkets, and industrial manufacturing/processing plants.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The following documents are required for new tax assessment or annual renewal:
- Duly completed **Trade Tax Assessment Return Form** (Annual Self-Declaration Form issued by the Revenue Division).
- Certified copy of the **Business Name Registration Certificate (Form 48/BR Certificate)** issued by the Divisional Secretariat.
- Copy of the proprietor's/managing director's **National Identity Card (NIC)**.
- **Financial Declaration / Certified Turnover Statement** (audited accounts or a self-certified statement of gross annual turnover) for businesses assessed on a variable/percentage-of-turnover bracket, rather than a fixed slab.
- Prior year's final **Trade Tax Payment Receipt** (for existing business renewals).
- Proof of Business Premises Address: Most recent **Assessment Rate Bill (Varipanam)** of the business premises or a copy of the Registered Lease Agreement.

## 4. Assessment & Payment Steps
- **Step 1: Receipt of Demand or Self-Assessment Filing**
    **Existing Businesses:** Receive the Annual Trade Tax Demand Notice sent by the Revenue Division prior to the tax year commencement.
    **New Businesses:** Must submit the completed Trade Tax Assessment Return Form at the Rates & Taxes Section immediately upon commencing commercial operations.

- **Step 2: Physical Verification & Financial Evaluation**
    The area Revenue Inspector conducts an on-site inspection to verify the nature of the business, the floor area utilized, the number of employees, and the visible scale of operations. If the business falls under a variable (turnover-based) tax slab, the Revenue Officer scrutinizes the submitted financial declaration.

- **Step 3: Final Tax Bill Generation**
    Based on the inspection report and the financial tier, the Assessment Division issues the finalized **Trade Tax Bill**, stating the exact assessed amount, the assessment year, and the payment due date.

- **Step 4: Tax Payment**
    The taxpayer proceeds to the Local Authority Shroff Counter to make the payment via cash, cheque, or bank draft. Alternatively, payment can be made through the council's authorized online payment gateway or partner bank deposit slip.

- **Step 5: Issuance of Receipt & Tax Clearance**
    Upon successful payment, the Shroff issues an official computer-generated fiscal receipt. This receipt serves as proof of payment for the financial year. A **Tax Clearance Stamp** may be affixed to the Trade License or a separate Tax Paid Certificate issued upon request.

## 5. Fees, Discounts & Penalties
- **Tax Rates:** The annual trade tax levy is set by the Council By-laws and varies significantly by tier:
    - **Tier A (Small Scale):** LKR 1,000 to LKR 5,000 per annum.
    - **Tier B (Medium Scale):** LKR 5,000 to LKR 20,000 per annum.
    - **Tier C (Large Scale/Corporate):** LKR 20,000 to LKR 50,000+ per annum (banks, large hotels, private hospitals).
    *(Variable rates based on a percentage of gross turnover apply to specified entities like financial institutions, subject to a council-fixed ceiling.)*
- **Early Payment Discount:** Some councils offer a 5% to 10% rebate on the tax amount if full payment is made before the end of January of the assessment year.
- **Payment Deadlines & Surcharges:**
    - **Standard Due Date:** March 31st of the current assessment year.
    - **Late Penalty:** A 10% surcharge is immediately applied to payments made after March 31st. Persistent default beyond the financial year may incur a cumulative penalty of up to 20% and recovery via warrant under the Municipal Councils Ordinance.
- **Standard SLA / Timeline:**
    - **Payment & Receipt Issuance:** Instant over-the-counter upon cash/cheque clearance.
    - **New Trade Tax Assessment & Tier Assignment:** 2 to 5 working days from the date of Revenue Inspector inspection.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division:** Tax & Revenue Department (Trade Tax & Business Assessment Unit).
- **Key Officers:** Revenue Inspector / Municipal Assessor / Municipal Treasurer (Finance Officer).
- **Contact Email:** tradetax@council.gov.lk
- **Office Hours:** Weekdays (Monday to Friday), 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM. The Shroff Counter closes for cash transactions at 3:00 PM sharp.
- **Escalation Grievance:** If a business disputes the tier classification or the assessed tax quantum, a formal written appeal for re-assessment must be filed with the **Municipal Finance Committee** through the **Municipal Treasurer** within 30 days of receiving the Tax Demand Notice. Failure to pay the undisputed portion of the tax during the appeal period will result in the accrual of penalty surcharges.
