# Assessment Rates (Property Tax) - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: Assessment and collection of quarterly county property taxes (commonly known as *Assessment Rates*) levied on all residential, commercial, industrial, and vacant properties situated within the administrative limits of Riverside County. This recurring tax is the primary revenue source for the County, funding services such as road maintenance, waste collection, and street lighting.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances**:
    - Riverside County Charter, Sections 230–245.
    - Riverside County Land Use Ordinance, Sections 225–240.
    - California Revenue and Taxation Code, Sections 147–162.
    - Assessment and Valuation Regulations (Annual Value determination based on the gross annual rental value as per the relevant ordinance).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who is liable**:
    - Registered property owners (as per the Assessment Roll) of lands and/or buildings (residential, commercial, vacant) located within County limits.
    - In cases where the owner is non-resident or untraceable, the legal occupier or tenant in actual possession of the property is deemed liable for payment, as provided for under the respective governing ordinances.
- **Property Types Assessed**: All immovable property unless specifically exempted by statute (e.g., places of public worship, public cemeteries, government buildings used for public purposes).

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
- **For Standard Quarterly Payment**:
    - Current or previous Assessor's Parcel Number (APN) (warrant number/bill number, visible on the top right of the physical rate notice).
    - Prior payment receipt if verifying arrears manually.
- **For Change of Ownership/Name Update in Assessment Roll**:
    - Copy of Owner's Driver's License or State ID number or Company Registration Certificate.
    - Certified copy of the Grant Deed / Deed of Gift.
    - Completed "Notice of Change in Ownership" form (available at the Tax Collector's Counter) or a written request letter for mutation of ownership.
- **For Remission of Rates for Vacant/Unoccupied Properties**:
    - Duly filled "Vacancy Remission Application" form (obtained from the County Assessor's Office).
    - Written notification letter from the property owner confirming the period of non-occupancy, submitted **prior to** or within the first week of the vacancy period.
    - Field Inspection Report confirming vacancy and lack of rental income, verified and endorsed by the Environmental Health Specialist or Building Inspector of the area.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Obtain Current Demand Notice**
    - Collect the quarterly rate bill physically delivered to your premises by the County's Tax Compliance Officer.
    - If not received, request a duplicate demand statement by visiting the Tax Collector's Counter or downloading it via Riverside County's digital portal/mobile app.
- **Step 2: Verify Dues and Calculations**
    - Clarify the breakdown with the Tax Collector's Counter Clerk: Check the Current Quarter Dues, any outstanding Prior Year Arrears, and the applicable incentive discount (e.g., 10% early settlement) or overdue surcharge calculations.
    - For vacant property remission requests, confirm the status of the application with the County Assessor's Office before payment to avoid overpayment.
- **Step 3: Execute Payment**
    - **Physical Payment**: Submit the bill at the County Cashier's Office. Tender payment via **Cash** or **Debit/Credit Card** (if Point-of-Sale facilities are available at the specific office).
    - **Digital Payment**: Log in to the County's official online payment portal or partner bank gateway, enter the Assessor's Parcel Number, and proceed via online banking or card payment.
- **Step 4: Receipt Collection & Validation**
    - Obtain the official computer-generated County Receipt (Form No. 27 or equivalent).
    - Validate the receipt contains: Owner Name, Assessor's Parcel Number, Premises Address, Payment Quarter, Amount Paid, and System Transaction ID.
    - **Critical**: Safeguard this receipt as legal proof of payment for ownership transfers, tax clearance certifications, and court litigation regarding property ownership.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Rates Percentage (Assessment Rate)**:
    - Calculated as a fixed percentage against the **Annual Rental Value** (Annual Value) of the property as determined by the County Assessor.
    - Standard rates typically range from **6% to 15%** depending on the County ordinances and property type (Commercial properties generally attract the upper limit).
- **Incentive Discounts (Subsidies)**:
    - **Full-Year Advance Payment**: A **10% discount** on the total annual rate if the entire year's tax is paid in full on or before **January 31st** of the assessment year.
    - **Prompt Quarterly Payment**: A **5% discount** if the specific quarterly sum is settled within the **first month** (i.e., January, April, July, or October) of the relevant quarter.
- **Late Payment Surcharge (Penalty)**:
    - A **10% surcharge** (penalty) is automatically added to the outstanding quarterly amount if payment is not received by the quarterly due date stipulated in the notice (typically the last day of the quarter).
- **SLA / Processing Timeline**:
    - **Counter Payment**: Immediate receipt generation.
    - **Online Payment**: Digital acknowledgment instant; official e-receipt generated within 24 hours; system record updated within 1 business day.
    - **Name Mutation (Ownership Change)**: 14–21 working days post-submission of complete documents and field verification.

## 6. Official Contact & Escalation
- **Department Division**: Riverside County Assessor's Office / Treasurer-Tax Collector, Tax & Revenue Department.
- **Primary Contact Point**: County Community Liaison (for initial assessment queries) or the County Cashier's Office.
- **Escalation Hierarchy**:
    1.  Tax Collector's Counter Clerk / Tax Compliance Officer.
    2.  Chief Cashier / Revenue Supervisor.
    3.  County Assessor / Deputy Executive Officer (Revenue).
    4.  Riverside County Executive Officer.
- **Contact Email**: `taxcollector@riversidecounty.gov`
- **Office Hours**: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Counter transactions typically close at 4:30 PM). Closed on federal holidays.
