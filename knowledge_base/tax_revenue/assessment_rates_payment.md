# Assessment Rates (Varipanam) - Tax & Revenue Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: Assessment and collection of quarterly municipal property taxes (commonly known as *Varipanam* or Rates) levied on all residential, commercial, industrial, and vacant properties situated within the administrative limits of the Local Authority. This recurring tax is the primary revenue source for the Council, funding municipal services such as road maintenance, waste collection, and street lighting.
- **Governing Laws & Ordinances**:
    - Municipal Councils Ordinance (Chapter 252), Sections 230–245.
    - Urban Councils Ordinance (Chapter 255), Sections 225–240.
    - Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987, Sections 147–162.
    - Assessment and Valuation Regulations (Annual Value determination based on the gross annual rental value as per the relevant ordinance).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Who is liable**:
    - Registered property owners (as per the Assessment Register) of lands and/or buildings (residential, commercial, vacant) located within the Council limits.
    - In cases where the owner is non-resident or untraceable, the legal occupier or tenant in actual possession of the property is deemed liable for payment, as provided for under the respective governing ordinances.
- **Property Types Assessed**: All immovable property unless specifically exempted by statute (e.g., places of public worship, public burial grounds, government buildings used for public purposes).

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
- **For Standard Quarterly Payment**:
    - Current or previous Property Assessment Account Number (warrant number/bill number, visible on the top right of the physical rate notice).
    - Prior payment receipt if verifying arrears manually.
- **For Change of Ownership/Name Update in Assessment Register**:
    - Copy of Owner's National Identity Card (NIC) or Company Registration Certificate.
    - Certified copy of the Title Deed of Transfer/Deed of Gift.
    - Completed “Notice of Transfer” form (available at the Rates Counter) or a written request letter for mutation of ownership.
- **For Remission of Rates for Vacant/Unoccupied Properties**:
    - Duly filled “Vacancy Remission Application” form (obtained from the Assessor's Department).
    - Written notification letter from the property owner confirming the period of non-occupancy, submitted **prior to** or within the first week of the vacancy period.
    - Field Inspection Report confirming vacancy and lack of rental income, verified and endorsed by the Public Health Inspector (PHI) or Technical Officer of the area.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Obtain Current Demand Notice**
    - Collect the quarterly rate bill physically delivered to your premises by the Council’s Revenue Inspector.
    - If not received, request a duplicate demand statement by visiting the Rates Counter (Shroff Wing) or downloading it via the local authority’s digital portal/mobile app.
- **Step 2: Verify Dues and Calculations**
    - Clarify the breakdown with the Rates Counter Clerk: Check the Current Quarter Dues, any outstanding Prior Year Arrears, and the applicable incentive discount (e.g., 10% early settlement) or overdue surcharge calculations.
    - For vacant property remission requests, confirm the status of the application with the Assessor’s Division before payment to avoid overpayment.
- **Step 3: Execute Payment**
    - **Physical Payment**: Submit the bill at the Shroff Counter. Tender payment via **Cash** or **Debit/Credit Card** (if Point-of-Sale facilities are available at the specific LA).
    - **Digital Payment**: Log in to the council’s official online payment portal or partner bank gateway, enter the Assessment Number, and proceed via Internet Banking or Card payment.
- **Step 4: Receipt Collection & Validation**
    - Obtain the official computer-generated Council Receipt (Form No. 27 or equivalent).
    - Validate the receipt contains: Owner Name, Assessment No., Premises Address, Payment Quarter, Amount Paid, and System Transaction ID.
    - **Critical**: Safeguard this receipt as legal proof of payment for ownership transfers, tax clearance certifications, and court litigation regarding property ownership.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Rates Percentage (Assessment Rate)**:
    - Calculated as a fixed percentage against the **Annual Rental Value** (Annual Value) of the property as determined by the Council Assessor.
    - Standard rates typically range from **6% to 15%** depending on the local by-laws and property type (Commercial properties generally attract the upper limit).
- **Incentive Discounts (Subsidies)**:
    - **Full-Year Advance Payment**: A **10% discount** on the total annual rate if the entire year’s tax is paid in full on or before **January 31st** of the assessment year.
    - **Prompt Quarterly Payment**: A **5% discount** if the specific quarterly sum is settled within the **first month** (i.e., January, April, July, or October) of the relevant quarter.
- **Late Payment Surcharge (Penalty)**:
    - A **10% surcharge** (penalty) is automatically added to the outstanding quarterly amount if payment is not received by the quarterly due date stipulated in the notice (typically the last day of the quarter).
- **SLA / Processing Timeline**:
    - **Counter Payment**: Immediate receipt generation.
    - **Online Payment**: Digital acknowledgment instant; official e-receipt generated within 24 hours; system record updated within 1 business day.
    - **Name Mutation (Ownership Change)**: 14–21 working days post-submission of complete documents and field verification.
