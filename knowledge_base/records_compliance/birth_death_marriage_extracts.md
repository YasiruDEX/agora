# Birth, Death & Marriage Certificate Extracts - Records & Compliance Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** Official issuance of certified true copies or computer-extracted prints of registered Birth, Death, and Marriage certificates from the vital records registers maintained at the Riverside County Recorder's Office. These certified extracts serve as primary legal documents for establishing identity, citizenship, succession, marital status, and familial lineage required for passport issuance, school admissions, state ID applications, property transfers, pension claims, and judicial proceedings.
- **Governing Laws & Regulations:**
    - California Health and Safety Code, Division 102 (Vital Records), and subsequent amendments.
    - California Family Code, Division 3 (Marriage), for General Marriages.
    - California Probate Code provisions on succession and estate administration relevant to death record requests.
    - Riverside County Recorder's Office Circulars and Directives governing the County Vital Records Database (e-CVRD) access and certified copy issuance protocols.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Birth Certificate Extract:** The individual named on the certificate, parent(s) named therein, legal guardian (with guardianship proof), or a direct descendant with legitimate interest. Authorized legal representatives must produce a notarized Power of Attorney or proxy.
- **Death Certificate Extract:** The surviving spouse, a child, parent, or immediate next of kin of the deceased. Estate administrators/executors or legal representatives handling estate matters may also apply with documentation establishing their interest.
- **Marriage Certificate Extract:** Either spouse named on the certificate, a legal child of the marriage, or an authorized legal representative.
- **Jurisdiction:** The application should be submitted to the Riverside County Recorder's Office branch where the original event was registered. However, under the centralized e-CVRD digital system, certified extracts for digitally archived records may be obtained from any Riverside County Recorder's Office branch (Riverside, Corona, Moreno Valley, Temecula, or Palm Desert) connected to the central database, subject to the issuing Deputy Recorder's verification protocol.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The applicant must produce the following:
- Duly completed **Application Form** specific to the certificate type:
    - **Form VR-63** for Birth Certificate Extracts.
    - **Form VR-63A** for Death Certificate Extracts.
    - **Form VR-121** for Marriage Certificate Extracts.
- Clear copy of the applicant's **Driver's License or State ID number**, valid Passport, or other government-issued photo ID. The original must be presented for visual verification.
- **Registration Particulars:** Accurate details of the original registration, including the Riverside County Recorder's Office branch of registration, Registration Entry Number, Book/Volume Number, and exact Date of Event. Providing these details eliminates the need for a manual search.
- **Search Request (Form VR-63 - Search Section):** If the exact entry number or date is unknown, the applicant must submit a separate Form VR-63 search request indicating the estimated year range (e.g., 1975-1980) for register scrutiny.
- **Authorization Documents (if applicable):**
    - A signed letter of authorization from the person entitled to obtain the certificate.
    - A copy of the authorized representative's Driver's License or State ID number.
    - In legal succession cases, a certified copy of the Letters Testamentary or Letters of Administration.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition**
    Obtain the relevant application form (Form VR-63, VR-63A, or VR-121) from the Vital Records Division counter at the Riverside County Recorder's Office or download it from the official web portal (www.riversidecounty.gov/recorder).

- **Step 2: Form Completion & Document Attachment**
    Complete the form legibly, furnishing exact registered names (as entered in the original register), registration branch, and approximate year. Attach the ID copy and any supporting authorization documents.

- **Step 3: Fee Payment at Cashier Counter**
    Submit the application at the designated Vital Records Counter for verification of completeness. The verifying clerk will calculate and endorse the search fee (if applicable) and certified copy fee. Proceed to the Cashier Counter to pay the prescribed fees and retain the official receipt.

- **Step 4: Record Retrieval & Verification**
    The Record Keeper / Recording Clerk retrieves the record from either the e-CVRD electronic database or the physical bound registers stored in the records vault. The clerk verifies that the applicant details match the record and checks for any endorsements (e.g., adoption notations, late registration orders, divorce decrees).

- **Step 5: Certification & Endorsement**
    The prepared extract is presented to the Deputy Recorder, who serves as the **Assistant County Recorder**. The Deputy Recorder certifies the copy as a true extract, applies the official seal and signature, and records the issuance in the Certificate Issue Register.

- **Step 6: Issuance & Collection**
    The applicant or authorized representative collects the signed and sealed certified extract upon production of the cashier receipt and original ID. Applicants who opted for mail delivery will have the certificate dispatched via Certified Mail to the address provided in the application.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Certified Copy Fee (Details Known):** $17 to $32 per single certified extract copy.
- **Search Fee (Details Unknown):** $15 to $40 per five-year block or register year searched. This fee is non-refundable even if the record is not located.
- **Postal Delivery Fee (Optional):** Standard Certified Mail postage (typically $6 to $12 depending on weight).
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:**
    - **Counter Service (Digital/e-CVRD record with exact details):** Same day to within 48 hours.
    - **Manual Search (Archived physical registers):** 3 to 7 working days. Applications requiring search of pre-1960 registers or fragmented records may require up to 14 working days.
