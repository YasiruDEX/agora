# Birth, Death & Marriage Certificate Extracts - Records & Compliance Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description:** Official issuance of certified true copies or computer-extracted prints of registered Birth, Death, and Marriage certificates from the civil registration registers maintained at the Divisional Secretariat. These certified extracts serve as primary legal documents for establishing identity, citizenship, succession, marital status, and familial lineage required for passport issuance, school admissions, national identity card applications, property transfers, pension claims, and judicial proceedings.
- **Governing Laws & Regulations:**
    - Births and Deaths Registration Act (Chapter 110) and subsequent amendments.
    - Marriage Registration Ordinance (Chapter 112) for General Marriages.
    - Kandyan Marriage and Divorce Act (Chapter 132) and Muslim Marriage and Divorce Act (Chapter 134) for respective personal law marriages.
    - Registrar General's Department (RGD) Circulars and Directives governing e-RGD database access and certified copy issuance protocols.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Birth Certificate Extract:** The individual named on the certificate, parent(s) named therein, legal guardian (with guardianship proof), or a direct descendant with legitimate interest. Authorized legal representatives must produce a stamped Power of Attorney or proxy.
- **Death Certificate Extract:** The surviving spouse, a child, parent, or immediate next of kin of the deceased. Succession administrators/executors or legal representatives handling estate matters may also apply with documentation establishing their interest.
- **Marriage Certificate Extract:** Either spouse named on the certificate, a legal child of the marriage, or an authorized legal representative.
- **Jurisdiction:** The application should be submitted to the Divisional Secretariat where the original event was registered. However, under the e-RGD centralized digital system, certified extracts for digitally archived records may be obtained from any Divisional Secretariat connected to the central database, subject to the issuing Divisional Secretary's verification protocol.

## 3. Required Documents (Checklist)
The applicant must produce the following:
- Duly completed **Application Form** specific to the certificate type:
    - **Form 63** for Birth Certificate Extracts.
    - **Form 63A** for Death Certificate Extracts.
    - **Form 121** for Marriage Certificate Extracts.
- Clear copy of the applicant's **National Identity Card (NIC)**, valid Passport, or Driving License. The original must be presented for visual verification.
- **Registration Particulars:** Accurate details of the original registration, including the Divisional Secretariat Division of registration, Registration Entry Number, Book/Volume Number, and exact Date of Event. Providing these details eliminates the need for a manual search.
- **Search Request (Form 63 - Search Section):** If the exact entry number or date is unknown, the applicant must submit a separate Form 63 search request indicating the estimated year range (e.g., 1975-1980) for register scrutiny.
- **Authorization Documents (if applicable):**
    - A signed letter of authorization from the person entitled to obtain the certificate.
    - A copy of the authorized representative's NIC.
    - In legal succession cases, a certified copy of the Grant of Probate or Letters of Administration.

## 4. Application Process & Steps
- **Step 1: Form Acquisition**
    Obtain the relevant application form (Form 63, 63A, or 121) from the Civil Registration Division counter at the Divisional Secretariat or download it from the Registrar General's Department official web portal (www.rgd.gov.lk).

- **Step 2: Form Completion & Document Attachment**
    Complete the form legibly, furnishing exact registered names (as entered in the original register), registration division, and approximate year. Attach the NIC copy and any supporting authorization documents.

- **Step 3: Fee Payment at Shroff**
    Submit the application at the designated Civil Registration Counter for verification of completeness. The verifying clerk will calculate and endorse the search fee (if applicable) and certified copy fee. Proceed to the Shroff Counter to pay the prescribed fees and retain the official receipt.

- **Step 4: Record Retrieval & Verification**
    The Record Keeper / Subject Clerk retrieves the record from either the electronic e-RGD database or the physical bound registers stored in the strong room. The clerk verifies that the applicant details match the record and checks for any endorsements (e.g., adoption notations, late registration orders, divorce decrees).

- **Step 5: Certification & Endorsement**
    The prepared extract is presented to the Divisional Secretary, who serves as the **Additional District Registrar**. The Divisional Secretary certifies the copy as a true extract, applies the official seal and signature, and records the issuance in the Certificate Issue Register.

- **Step 6: Issuance & Collection**
    The applicant or authorized representative collects the signed and sealed certified extract upon production of the Shroff receipt and original NIC. Applicants who opted for postal delivery will have the certificate dispatched via Registered Post to the address provided in the application.

## 5. Fees & Timelines
- **Certified Copy Fee (Details Known):** LKR 100 to LKR 200 per single certified extract copy.
- **Search Fee (Details Unknown):** LKR 100 to LKR 500 per five-year block or register year searched. This fee is non-refundable even if the record is not located.
- **Postal Delivery Fee (Optional):** Standard inland Registered Post postage (typically LKR 50 to LKR 150 depending on weight).
- **Standard SLA / Processing Timeline:**
    - **Counter Service (Digital/e-RGD record with exact details):** Same day to within 48 hours.
    - **Manual Search (Archived physical registers):** 3 to 7 working days. Applications requiring search of pre-1960 registers or fragmented records may require up to 14 working days.
