# Public Complaints & Grievance Redressal - Records & Compliance Department

## 1. Overview
- **Service Description**: A structured and legally mandated mechanism enabling residents to formally lodge complaints, report public nuisances, highlight county infrastructure failures, appeal against administrative delays, report staff misconduct, and seek redress for service deficiencies. This mechanism ensures accountability through documented tracking, investigation, and resolution within prescribed timelines under the Riverside County Citizen Charter framework.
- **Governing Laws & Directives**:
    - California Public Records Act (CPRA), Government Code Section 7920 et seq.
    - Riverside County Administrative Circulars on Citizen Charters and Public Grievance Handling (issued by the Riverside County Executive Office).
    - Riverside County Code of Ordinances / California Government Code provisions on County Boards and Special Districts.
    - Riverside County Municipal Code provisions on Nuisance, Business Licensing, and Public Health.
    - Riverside County Ombudsman's Office Charter Provisions.

## 2. Scope of Serviceable Grievances
- **County & Infrastructure Issues**:
    - Blocked stormwater drains, culverts, and flood-control channels causing flooding.
    - Damaged, potholed, or poorly maintained county roads and sidewalks.
    - Non-functional or damaged streetlights (report with pole number).
    - Unauthorized permanent/temporary structures, road encroachments, and illegal signage.
    - Water supply disruptions managed by the County (standpipes, water tankers).
- **Public Health & Environmental Nuisance**:
    - Missed or irregular household/commercial trash collection.
    - Illegal dumping of solid waste, construction debris, or hazardous material on public/vacant lands.
    - Mosquito breeding sites (stagnant water, discarded containers, blocked drains) posing West Nile virus risk.
    - Noise pollution from commercial establishments, workshops, places of worship, or public events.
    - Stray animal hazards (loose dogs, livestock roaming on public roads).
    - Insanitary conditions in food premises, bakeries, or markets.
- **Business & Administrative Complaints**:
    - Illegal business operations without a valid Business License or Environmental Health Permit.
    - Violation of business license conditions (operating beyond permitted hours, unauthorized change of use).
    - Excessive delays in processing applications for permits, licenses, or plan approvals beyond the stated Citizen Charter SLA.
    - Officer misconduct, negligence, discourtesy, or solicitation of illegal gratification.
    - Non-compliance by the County with its published Citizen Charter service commitments.

## 3. Required Details & Documentation (Checklist)
To ensure prompt action and verifiable tracking, complainants are requested to provide the following:
- **Complainant Identity**:
    - Full Name (as per ID).
    - Driver's License or State ID number.
    - Residential Address (within or outside county limits).
    - Valid Contact Telephone Number (Mobile preferred for SMS updates) and/or Email Address.
    - *Note on Anonymity*: Anonymous complaints are accepted and acted upon for severe public health threats (e.g., active mosquito breeding sites, illegal dumping of hazardous material) or corruption allegations. However, tracking updates will not be available without verified contact details.
- **Incident/Issue Details**:
    - Precise location/address of the issue (including nearest landmark, Supervisorial District, or GPS pin if available).
    - Date and time the problem was first observed.
    - Clear narrative description of the grievance.
- **Supporting Evidence**:
    - Photographs or short video clips clearly depicting the problem (digital evidence is highly encouraged).
    - Previous Complaint Reference Number(s) if this is a recurring or re-opened issue.
    - Copies of any prior written correspondence or receipts submitted to the County regarding the same matter.

## 4. Submission & Redressal Steps
- **Step 1: Lodging the Complaint** (via any of the following official channels):
    - **In-Person**: Visit the Public Complaints Counter / Citizen Helpdesk during office hours. A designated officer will transcribe or receive the written complaint.
    - **Digital/Email**: Submit via the official Riverside County website portal, dedicated mobile application, or direct email to the Grievance Unit.
    - **Telephone**: Call the Riverside County Information Center (311) / Grievance Unit Helpline. The operator logs the complaint into the system.
    - **Written Letter**: Sent by certified mail addressed to the County Clerk / County Executive Officer.
    - *CPRA Note*: If the complaint pertains to accessing information not available publicly, it must be filed as a formal public records request under the California Public Records Act with the applicable fee and designated Public Information Officer.
- **Step 2: Acknowledgement & Registration**:
    - Upon verification of basic details, the complaint is registered in the Central Grievance Database.
    - A unique **Complaint Reference Number (e.g., COM/RC/2026/01234)** and an Acknowledgement Receipt are generated and issued instantly to the complainant via SMS, Email, or physical slip.
- **Step 3: Triage & Assignment**:
    - The Public Complaints Officer/Compliance Unit reviews the complaint and assigns it to the relevant Subject Division Head:
        - *Environmental Health Division*: Public health nuisances, vector control, food safety.
        - *Public Works/Engineering Division*: Roads, drains, streetlights, buildings, water.
        - *Waste Management Division*: Trash collection, illegal dumping.
        - *Revenue/Business Licensing Division*: Unauthorized business activity, license violations.
        - *Internal Audit/Administration*: Staff misconduct, service delays.
- **Step 4: Field Investigation & Inspection**:
    - The assigned officer (e.g., Environmental Health Specialist, Public Works Inspector, Code Enforcement Officer, Revenue Inspector) conducts a mandatory site visit within the SLA timeline.
    - The officer verifies the facts, assesses the severity, and photographs the site.
- **Step 5: Action & Enforcement**:
    - **For Public Works**: The officer issues a work order to the relevant crew or contractor for rectification (e.g., drain clearing, pothole patching).
    - **For Nuisance/Encroachment**: A formal **Enforcement Notice** is served to the offending party (e.g., a 7-day notice to abate nuisance under the Riverside County Code/Municipal Code). Failure to comply leads to legal action in the Superior Court.
- **Step 6: Closure & Notification**:
    - Upon completion of remedial action or enforcement, the officer updates the case status in the database as "Resolved" with detailed remarks.
    - The complainant is notified of the resolution via SMS, Email, or formal letter, referencing the original Complaint Reference Number. The complainant is provided a window to file an appeal if dissatisfied with the resolution.

## 5. SLAs, Timelines & Escalation
- **Acknowledgement SLA**:
    - Counter / Hotline / Online Portal: **Immediate** (instant reference number).
    - Email / Written Postal: Within **1 working day** of receipt.
- **Emergency / Priority 1 (Public Health & Life Safety)**:
    - *Examples*: Active mosquito breeding sites, burst water/sewer mains, road cave-ins, dangerous structures, livestock on major roads.
    - *Action Initiation*: Within **24 to 48 hours**.
- **Standard Infrastructure & Nuisance (Priority 2)**:
    - *Examples*: Blocked drains, missed trash collection, potholes, illegal dumping, streetlight failure, noise complaint, illegal construction.
    - *Investigation & Initial Response*: Within **5 to 14 working days**. Final resolution timeline communicated based on complexity.
- **Complaint Closure**:
    - Target total resolution: **30 working days** for standard cases.
- **Escalation Process (If Unresolved Within SLA)**:
    - **Level 1**: Escalate to the **Public Complaints Officer / Head of Subject Division**.
    - **Level 2**: Escalate to the **Internal Audit & Compliance Officer** for administrative review.
    - **Level 3**: Formal written appeal to the **County Executive Officer / County Clerk**.
    - **Level 4 (External)**: If exhausted within the county, escalate to the **Riverside County Ombudsman's Office** or the **California State Auditor's Whistleblower Hotline** for administrative and procedural injustice. The complainant retains the right to seek legal remedy.
