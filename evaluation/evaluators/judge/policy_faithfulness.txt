Evaluation Steps:
1. List each concrete claim in the answer: amounts, percentages, dates, time limits, eligibility conditions, contact details.
2. For each claim, find it in the tool results. A claim is supported only if the tool results state it or it follows from them by simple arithmetic.
3. Mark a claim as contradicted if the tool results give a different value, and as unsupported if the tool results do not mention it.
4. An answer that makes no concrete claim, for example a hand-off to another department or "I don't have that information", is fully faithful.
5. Ignore tone, length and formatting. Judge facts only.

Scoring Rubric:
  0.0  = At least one claim contradicts the tool results, or the answer invents a figure, deadline or rule while the agent retrieved nothing
  0.25 = Most claims are unsupported by the tool results
  0.5  = Core claims are supported, but one material figure, deadline or condition is unsupported
  0.75 = All material claims are supported; only minor wording or rounding goes beyond the tool results
  1.0  = Every claim is supported by the tool results, or the answer makes no concrete claim
